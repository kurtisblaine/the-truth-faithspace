Technology

Beware of Idols

 A brief encouragement about staying away from idols. In many ways, we are tricked into trading reality for a fake, unknowingly taking the shadow for the substance. And in the church as well: as it says, "no one is to act as your judge in regard to food or drink or in respect to a festival or a new moon or a Sabbath day— things which are a mere shadow of what is to come; but the substance belongs to Christ.” (Colossians 2:16-17) Likewise, men trading reality for a simulation, the real for a fake, are left dissatisfied with a thing which sold itself great, but is less. “For they exchanged the truth of God for a lie, and worshiped and served the creature rather than the Creator, who is blessed forever. Amen.” (Romans 1:25) For although a lie manifests itself, it doesn't make it true; but it shows itself as exceedingly sinful, for it attempts to promote its falsehood as true.

For this reason, I expound technology and the truth therein, showing how technology can be modern day idolatry. Not all technology is idolatry; but all technology is compromised of images. In ancient Israel, the men of Isael worshipped images of calves, images of snakes, yes, and images of their neighboring nation's gods. Today idolatry is not as primitive as it once was; for now idolatry shows itself in materialism, covetousness, greediness, and perhaps even, technology. For everything on a display of a computer is made up of pictures, snapshots, images. A video is made up of still frame shots which show at a high rate of speed, commonly refered to as fps, or frames per second; typically 60 fps is the rate a monitor renders images, which are frames. This is similar to how a flip book works: there are many images which change slightly as you progress throughout the book; when you flip through them, the still images seem animated.

The idea underlying idolatry is that a dead image is seemingly more: it is living, it is a god, it is to be revered - an inanimate object represented in the form of a god. If that image is smashed, would you be angered? Would you be offended that your god is ruined? Are you attributing more to an object than you ought? For pagans lift up their idols and dust them off and highly esteem them although they are nothing. For a Christian, we know there is no god but One, whom we serve; so why would we revere an object in the form of a god? Even within the church there have snuck in unbelievers who, not satisfied with faith, make up a god for themselves into a form by artist's imaginations. However the law says, "The LORD spoke to you from the midst of the fire; you heard the sound of words, but you saw no form—only a voice.” (Deuteronomy 4:12) For "You cannot see My face, for no man can see Me and live!" (Exodus 33:20)

Instead of clinging to God through faith in Jesus Christ, who is the visible image of the invisible God, for “ though you have not seen Him, you love Him, and though you do not see Him now, but believe in Him, you greatly rejoice with joy inexpressible and full of glory,” (1 Peter 1:8) for you worship Him as God through faith which is apart from sight. But not satisfied with faith by hearing, their faith is by seeing, which is not faith at all; for they invent statues, images, representations of the living Christ, and they stand them up in the church! "Show me your faith without the works, and I will show you my faith by my works." (James 2:18) And so: show me your faithlessness by your images. For faith is without sight; where there is sight, faith is made impossible; but our sight is through the knowledge of the Lord Jesus Christ, so that our faith may not be blind, so that we may truly see; they who profess faith through sight become blind to the faith, that they may not see the light of the glory of God in the face of Jesus Christ.
