Holy, Holy, Holy is He

My name is Kurtis Below is my testimony. Right before Covid-19, I had just gotten my full time job, experienced military life and college. "What is left, but to get married, have kids, and die?", I said to myself, “Surely life isn’t here, but where?” I started seeking. I figured mankind doesn’t know, if they did, wouldn’t they tell me? But all my life no-one told me the truth. I don't remember ever seeing a street preacher; nor do I remember anything spectacular in church. We were the family that went during the holidays and once in a while outside of that, but it wasn't anything spectacular to me - nothing stood out.

I seen the churches that were claiming God weren't trying to convince me of God, so they must not really know God - for the one who is convinced of God, convinces others. I also seen that it's very popular - on every street corner. I said to myself, “Surely if the truth exists, only a few would know it. The truth wouldn't be popular, would it?" For one, is not the truth a mystery? And two, isn't it painful? But yet, I believed truth existed and I searched for it.

I started to open my Bible; my mom had given it to me. She was on a journey herself with God and gave all her things to the poor. I said “What? This is from God?” Then she moved out of the state. That made me want to seek all the more. So I opened up my Bible and remembered the change it had on my life; although the change was quick, it wasn’t drastic: I had some peace and other spiritual things, but I was still in sin.

I remember doing much work at my new job and while working, I kept reading Scripture every morning. I remember praying to God, “Ah! If only I could seek You more.” I don’t know if this was an answer to my prayers, but then Covid-19 happened and I was put in solitude, working from home. I read and read, and in my reading, I prayed. The Scripture that opened my eyes up the most was the book of Ecclesiastes: I seen the wisdom of God and the futility of life. Also the Book of the Psalms: I had very much conviction because of my sin and knowledge of the righteous judgement of God - which made me hunger and thirst for righteousness all the more. And the book of Isaiah, the prophet: the revealing of my own wicked heart and the hope of redemption from it. The Scriptures were very bitter to me & like death because I didn't obey them - I tried, but I could not fully do. Sin was always there, but I hoped in God for redemption. I started at the beginning of the Bible and went on. I seen in my study notes, the Christ, but I did not know Him; the Scriptures spoke about Him, but I only knew Him by name.

All the reading through the Old Testament built me up to show me how great Christ is; also, it torn me down to show me how fallen I am - I see now this was so I would not hope in myself for salvation, but God alone. When I got to the Gospels, surely I seen the greatness of Jesus Christ. Firstly, the words of Him in my text were red. And He did many signs: the devils cried out, the dead were raised, His teachings with authority, and the miracles with power. But He died. Even stranger to me, He rose from the dead. I didn’t understand anything. He sends out His disciples...

I skipped the other three Gospels and went into the book of Acts. The disciples did the same things that He did: they preached, did miracles, and raised the dead. I still did not understand. All the while I was constantly getting on my knees to God in prayer because of my sin, knowing that my wicked deeds deserved wickedness. But I was persistent, knowing only God could save me. My sins did not stop me from coming to Him because I knew He was loving, kind and gracious. But the things which I did not want to do, I constantly did. I did not want to do it, but I did it anyway. I had no power in myself to say, "No", but sin was always there. Many times I thought God would strike me because of my wickedness, especially when I would confess my sins to Him; but actually, the opposite happened: I was relieved and felt better. But still, I couldn't stop sinning. I would go a few days without it, but I would always end up back in it.

I heard the apostle’s sermons about Christ, and seen the effect their word had on people: they burned their sorcery books, turned from idols, repented to Jesus. Paul, a preacher, once a persecutor of the church, turned to Him through revelation of the living Christ who appeared to Him; and all the apostles seen the risen Savior. However, the entire Gospel message wasn’t contained in the book of Acts, only a small excerpt of it - a little here and a little there, so I still didn’t understand. However, I sought Christ until I found Him; I said to myself, “There’s something about Jesus and I will not let go until I find Him.”

Then I read the book of Romans. The apostle Paul explains Christ. I could trust his testimony because the Lord revealed Himself to him on the road to Damascus. When reading, that’s when Christ came alive to me. I seen Christ came for the very problem I had: sin. I did not know He came to earth for me, nor did I understand He knew my problem. He came to deliver me from the very problem I had. He didn't expect me to figure it out on my own, but He did what I could not do: deliver. And because of the revelation of His love toward me, despite my present wickedness, I wept and wept - for I had not wept in years. I didn't know I had a problem, but He revealed my problem and took it away.

Romans 7:14-25 spoke to me directly for that was exactly what I was going through. It was like those very verses were put in there for me! I didn’t know what I was going through, but God did; and He showed me. For He knows me better than I know me. Only I know me. But if there is a God, He would know me too. He showed me that He knows me. And that, better than I know me. For the things in me I cannot explain. But He explains. And better than I could ever explain. He delivered me from that which I could not understand. I could not even understand my problem, how much less could I deliver myself? I believed nothing, until I believed one thing, and then I believed everything. For I said to myself, “If this is true, then so is everything else in this Book.” The one thing I believed is that Jesus Christ loved me and gave Himself up for me; and He proved Himself to be much alive.

From then, I didn’t cry out because of my sins anymore, but I had joy that He had forgiven me and delivered me from my sins - I had experienced deliverance. The sin I didn’t want to do, I did not do. The chains which shackled me fell off. I had a clear mind and I could see what was in there. As I went on, all the commands of the apostles were sweet to me, because I did them! I didn't need to be told to be full of joy, be thankful, sing to the Lord, live holy, tell others, study to show yourself approved, separate from evil - all these things I did because of my love for Him! But in me I also had an anger, "Why didn’t anyone tell me? This death I was living in and no-one warned me?" I went outside and everything I seen for years was new to my eyes - I seen God in everything. Everything was beautiful. The glory of the Lord filled the earth to my eyes; before I could see nothing.

And now, I have been studying to know more about Him and to know the Gospel. I know what He has done for me, He has done the same for all people. I know what has happened to me needs to be replicated in others. I wish for others to be saved; and everyone needs to know what Jesus has done for them. The knowledge of Him is everything we need. He teaches me through His Word how to speak and act for Him. I have experienced much discipline from the Lord, but I know it is for my good that I may be conformed to His image. He disciplines because of love, like a father disciplines his child. The more I learn of the goodness of Him, the more of the evil I see in me - He is so good. In repentance & peace I conform my life after His, following Him, not making for myself my own pathways or byways, but maintaining to His highway - I know His Way is straight and narrow and leads to eternal life. I was changed by God - and who can change me back? He is for me - who can be against me? If these things are true for me - a big sinner - how much for you? Where sin abounds, His grace abounds more; those who are forgiven much, love more. Glory be to the only wise God in heaven. Amen. Believe All of man's duty to God is found in this one thing. May God bless you and reveal the grace of His Son Jesus Christ to you and keep you from evil. We don't know where we came from - we can't say what we did a day ago; we don't know where we are going - we can't say what will happen tomorrow. All we know is today and all we know is right now. Today is the day of salvation; right now is the time of God's favor. When you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts as did the Israelites in their rebellion in the wilderness where they tested God for 40 years, although they seen His mighty works. Know this: the root of all disobedience and sin is unbelief; therefore, let us assure ourselves with the confidence which comes from faith in Jesus Christ - He died for us, He was buried, He was raised, and He now lives forever, seated at the right hand of the Father in heaven. Know surely what faith is: faith is sight without seeing, the conviction of things unseen, the assurance of things hoped for.

Let us remember the exhortations and exclamations spoken by the Lord to those who would hear and believe in Him, producing in themselves a very great faith, having themselves marveled the Lord by their faith, and calling to Him for deliverance because they believed He is powerful and able to do. As the examples are found in the Scriptures, And when Jesus entered Capernaum, a centurion came to Him, imploring Him, and saying, “Lord, my servant is lying paralyzed at home, fearfully tormented.” Jesus said to him, “I will come and heal him.” But the centurion said, “Lord, I am not worthy for You to come under my roof, but just say the word, and my servant will be healed. Now when Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those who were following, “Truly I say to you, I have not found such great faith with anyone in Israel. And, But He answered and said, “I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.” But she came and began to bow down before Him, saying, “Lord, help me!” And He answered and said, “It is not good to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.” But she said, “Yes, Lord; but even the dogs feed on the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.” Then Jesus said to her, “O woman, your faith is great; it shall be done for you as you wish.” And her daughter was healed at once.
Let us, on the other hand, remember the Lord's words to those who doubted in times past and let us not shrink back in disbelief and fear, but instead be strengthen by God's grace moving forward, fearlessly, towards the heavenly prize which is ours in Christ Jesus. He reprimanded many because of their little faith, as the examples show, if God so clothes the grass of the field, which is alive today and tomorrow is thrown into the furnace, will He not much more clothe you? You of little faith! And, And behold, there arose a great storm on the sea, so that the boat was being covered with the waves; but Jesus Himself was asleep. And they came to Him and woke Him, saying, “Save us, Lord; we are perishing!” He said to them, "Why are you afraid, you men of little faith?” Then He got up and rebuked the winds and the sea, and it became perfectly calm. And, Peter said to Him, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water.” And He said, “Come!” And Peter got out of the boat, and walked on the water and came toward Jesus. But seeing the wind, he became frightened, and beginning to sink, he cried out, “Lord, save me!” Immediately Jesus stretched out His hand and took hold of him, and said to him, “You of little faith, why did you doubt?” And, When they came to the crowd, a man came up to Jesus, falling on his knees before Him and saying, “Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is a lunatic and is very ill; for he often falls into the fire and often into the water. I brought him to Your disciples, and they could not cure him.” And Jesus answered and said, "You unbelieving and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I put up with you?"
So we see how true it is: through faith we are saved, by the grace of our Lord Jesus the Savior. Finally, let us keep in mind, what is needed is not a faith that is grand, but a faith the size of a mere mustard seed. If Christ reprimanded them on their little faith, then how little that little faith must have been! Let us consider the prophet Elijah who is a human like us - he prayed that it would not rain and it did not rain for three and a half years. Then he prayed again for rain and it rained just as he asked. Let us beware of doubt and fear which are the killers of faith. Confusion is the product of being double-minded or impure from the blending together of falsehood and truth or this world with the Kingdom of God. The impure have no inheritence in the Kingdom of the Father, as it says in the Scriptures. So, let us take heed lest we become a waverer ourselves, as the things which happened before provide examples for us today, It has often thrown him both into the fire and into the water to destroy him. But if You can do anything, take pity on us and help us!” And Jesus said to him, “‘If You can?’ All things are possible to him who believes.” Immediately the boy’s father cried out and said, “I do believe; help my unbelief.” And, Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and said, “Why could we not drive it out?” And He said to them, “Because of the littleness of your faith; for truly I say to you, if you have faith the size of a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move; and nothing will be impossible to you.
While we believe the Word of God, we correct ourselves to it. And this is the way of life: from start to finish by faith in Jesus Christ - hearing the Word and correcting ourselves to Him. The one who disbelieves the warnings, goes astray; the one who hears and fails to practice what he hears, does likewise. As Wisdom says, For the commandment is a lamp and the teaching is light; And reproofs for discipline are the way of life And, He who keeps instruction is in the way of life, But he who refuses correction goes astray. Through faith, we gain knowledge of God and in Him, there is righteousness. No-one is righteous except the only God; so if anyone be moral, it's because of the morality that is in God through abiding in Him through Christ. No-one has seen God at anytime, but Jesus Christ has made Him known to us - He is the visible image of the invisble God. Through Christ, we can see the Father, imitating Him, not making for ourselves a righteousness of our own through the law, but that which is through faith in Jesus Christ; and if it's not by works, but by faith, then where is the boasting? As even Jesus said, Why do you call Me good? there is none good but one, that is, God. We correct ourselves to the Word, through rebuke and reproof and discipline; the wise listen to correction, but a fool departs from it, not heeding to the word. The Scriptures make one wise unto salvation, and through wisdom, we grow in the grace of the Lord; but a fool does not fear the Lord and says in His heart, "No God", departing from the way of life. In faith and through faith, we chose the fear of the Lord unto wisdom unto greater grace unto salvation; as it says, you have known the Holy Scriptures, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus. And, And Jesus increased in wisdom and stature, and in favor with God and men.
And this is the favor, the grace, the truth: God sees all of our heart and the wickedness therein; He hates sin and loves righteousness - in man, He sees no righteousness and He sees man has no fear of Him. Although seeing the depravity of mankind, He did not send forth a torrent to devour us, but instead, He sent forth His beloved Son - the Son whom He loves and favors - He sent Him forth not to judge the world, but to save it, He sent Him forth not for those who think they are righteous, but to those who know they are sinners, He sent forth not to bring peace, but a sword - and that of the truth which divides. Although seeing all of the sin of us, He sent forth His Son, on whom abides the great love of God, to die for our sins. He knew no sin, but took upon Himself our sin; He hates sin, but became that which He hates for those He loves. He did this, not while we deserved it, but while we least deserved it; when we were deserving of wrath, He gave us the grace of His Son; He loved us in the greatest way possible even while we were the greatest sinners possible. He knows our sins even while they are hidden from our sight, yet He died for us, and that to free us from sin and death. Nothing is hidden from His sight and He, seeing our hidden faults, sent His Son into the world to reveal the message of His grace - which is not by our works, but to be received through our faith. He being pure became dirty to make us - the dirty ones - clean; He takes away the sin of the world by bearing it upon Himself; as John, the Baptist, says, The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him, and said, “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world! And the apostle Paul, For the love of Christ controls us, having concluded this, that one died for all, therefore all died; And, For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all men, By grace we are saved, in none other than Jesus Christ, the only name under heaven by which men can be saved; by calling upon His name, crying out to Him - a faith that confesses, that truly believes, that searches and seeks for the living God despite not being able to see Him. The only wise God who is, was, and is yet to come! Come, Lord Jesus!
