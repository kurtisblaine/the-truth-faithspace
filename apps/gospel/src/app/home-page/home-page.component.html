Holy, Holy, Holy is He

My name is Kurtis Below is my testimony and a brief message of encouragement. Right before Covid-19, I had just gotten my full time job, experienced military life and college. "What is left, but to get married, have kids, and die?" I said to myself, “Surely life isn’t here, but where?” I started seeking. I figured mankind doesn’t know; if they did, wouldn’t they tell me? But all my life no-one told me the truth. I don't remember ever seeing a street preacher; nor do I remember anything spectacular in church. We were the family that went during the holidays and once in a while outside of that, but it wasn't anything spectacular to me - nothing stood out.

I seen the churches that were claiming God, weren't trying to convince me of God, so they must not really know - for the one who is convinced, convinces. I also seen that they were very popular - on every street corner. I said to myself, “Surely if truth exists, only a few would know it. The truth wouldn't be popular, would it?" For one, is not the truth a mystery? And two, isn't it painful? But yet, I believed the truth existed; I searched for it.

I started to open my Bible; my mom had given it to me. She was on a journey herself with God and gave all her things to the poor. I said “What? This is from God?” Then she moved out of the state. That made me want to seek all the more. So I opened up my Bible and remembered the change it had on my life; although the change was quick, it wasn’t drastic: I had some peace and other spiritual things, but I was still in sin.

I remember doing much work at my new job; while working, I kept reading Scripture every morning. I remember praying to God, “Ah! If only I could seek You more.” I don’t know if this was an answer to my prayers or not, but then Covid-19 happened and I was put in solitude, working from home. I read and read, and in my reading, I prayed. The Scripture that opened my eyes up the most was the book of Ecclesiastes: I seen the wisdom of God and the futility of life. Also the Book of the Psalms: I had very much conviction because of my sin and knowledge of the righteous judgement of God - which made me hunger and thirst for righteousness all the more. And the book of Isaiah, the prophet: the revealing of my own wicked heart and the hope of redemption from it. The Scriptures were very bitter to me & like death because I didn't obey - I tried, but I couldn't. Sin was always there, but I hoped in God for redemption. I started at the beginning of the Bible and went on. I seen in my study notes, the Christ, but I did not know Him; the Scriptures spoke about Him, but I only knew Him by name.

All the reading through the Old Testament showed me how great Christ was; also, it torn me down to show me how fallen I am - I see now this was so I would not hope in myself for salvation, but God alone. When I got to the Gospels, surely I seen the greatness of Jesus Christ. Firstly, the words of Him in my text were red. And He did many signs and miracles: the devils cried out, the dead were raised, His teachings with authority, and the miracles with power. But He died. Even stranger to me, He rose from the dead. I didn’t understand anything. He sends out His disciples...

I skipped the other three Gospels and went into the book of Acts. The disciples did the same things that He did: they preached, did miracles, and raised the dead. I still did not understand. All the while I was constantly getting on my knees to God in prayer because of my sin, knowing that my wicked deeds deserved wickedness. But I was persistent - knowing only God could save me. My sins did not stop me from coming to Him because I knew He was loving, kind and gracious. But the things which I did not want to do, I constantly did. I did not want to do it, but I did it anyway. I had no power in myself to say, "No"; but sin was always there. Many times I thought God would strike me because of my wickedness, especially when I would confess my sins to Him; but actually, the opposite happened: I was relieved and felt better. But still, I couldn't stop sinning. I would go a few days without it, but I would always end up back in it.

I heard the apostle’s sermons about Christ, and seen the effect their word had on people: they burned their sorcery books, turned from idols, repented to Jesus. Paul, a preacher, once a persecutor of the church, turned to Him through revelation of the living Christ who appeared to Him! Besides him, all the other apostles seen the risen Savior. However, the entire Gospel message wasn’t contained in the book of Acts, only a small excerpt of it - a little here and a little there, so I still didn’t understand. However, I sought Christ until I found Him; I said to myself, “There’s something about Jesus and I will not let go until I find it.”

Then I read the book of Romans. The apostle Paul explains Christ. I could trust his testimony because the Lord revealed Himself on the road to Damascus, electing him to the work of spreading the Gospel. When reading, that’s when Christ came alive to me. I seen Christ came for the very problem I had: sin. I did not know He came to earth for me, nor did I understand He knew my problem. He came to deliver me from the very problem I had. He didn't expect me to figure it out on my own, but He did what I could not do: deliver. And because of the revelation of His love toward me, despite my present wickedness, I wept and wept - for I had not wept in years. I didn't know I had a problem, but He revealed the problem and took it away.

Romans 7:14-25 spoke to me directly for that was exactly what I was going through. It was like those very verses were put in there for me. I didn’t know what I was going through, but God did; and He showed me. For He knows me better than I know me. Only I know me. But if there is a God, He would know me too. He shows me that He knows me. And that, better than I know me. For the things in me I cannot explain. But He explains. And better than I could ever explain. He delivered me from that which I could not understand. I could not even understand my issue, how much less could I deliver myself? I believed nothing until I believed one thing, then I believed everything. For I said to myself, “If this is true, then so is everything else in this Book.” The one thing I believed is that Jesus Christ loved me and gave Himself up for me; and He proved Himself to be alive.

From then on, I didn’t cry out because of my sin anymore because I had joy that He had forgiven me and delivered me from my sins - I had experienced deliverance. The sin I didn’t want to do, I did not do. The chains which shackled me fell off. I had a clear mind and I could see what was in there. As I went on, all the commands of the apostles were sweet to me, because I did them! I didn't need to be told to be full of joy, be thankful, sing to the Lord, live holy, tell others, study to show yourself approved, separate from evil - all these things I did because of my love for Him! But in me I also had an anger, "Why didn’t anyone tell me? This death I was living in and no-one warned me?" I went outside and everything I seen for years was new to my eyes - I seen God in everything. Everything was beautiful. The glory of the Lord filled the earth to my eyes; before I could see nothing.

And now, I have been studying to know more about Him and to know the Gospel. I know what He has done for me, He has done for all people. I know what has happened to me needs to be replicated in others. I wish for others to be saved; and everyone needs to know what Jesus has done for them. The knowledge of Him is everything we need. He teaches me through His Word how to speak and act for Him. I have experienced much discipline from the Lord, but I know it is for my good - that I may be conformed to His image. He disciplines me as a father disciplines his son - to purge from me my inner-most folly. The more I learn of the goodness of Him, the more evil I see in me. In repentance & peace I conform my life after His, following Him, not making for myself a pathways of my own, but maintaining to His highway - I know His Way is straight and narrow and leads to eternal life.

I was changed by God - who can change me back? He is for me - who is against me? If these things are true for me, a big sinner, how much more for you? Where sin abounds, His grace abounds the more; those who are forgiven much, love the more. Glory be to the only wise God in heaven who redeemed us through His own blood, loving us in truth. Amen. Believe in the Son of God - Jesus Christ All of man's duty to God in found in this one thing. Greetings. May the Lord God bless you and keep you, revealing the grace of His Son, Jesus Christ, to you. We don't know where we came from - we can't say what we did a day ago; we don't know where we are going - we can't say what we will do tomorrow. All we know is today; all we know is right now. Today is the day of salvation; right now is the time of God's favor. When you hear the Spirit of His Word, do not harden yourself as the Israelites did in the rebellion, when they disobeyed in the wilderness, when they tested God for 40 years, although they seen His mighty works which were on display constantly before their eyes. And even in the wilderness, they seen the cloud covering Mount Sinai, but seen not the form of Him; as it says, You cannot see My face, for no man can see Me and live! They seen Moses enter the Tent of Meeting, and the cloud descended, but seen not God; they seen the pillar of fire by night and the pillar of cloud by day, but not the Holy one; they seen the manna from heaven and the water from the rock, but not Him who preformed these signs. For this is faith, this is belief: perception without seeing, the conviction of things unseen, the assurance of things hoped for.

And that latter generation, when Christ came, tested Him and tried Him, but He, graciously putting up with that generation for years, although His works were numerous amongst them - Yes, they were constantly seeing His works, yet trying Him, questioning His authority, not even believing John, who came before Him, nor Himself, seeking for a sign from heaven, seeing greater things than the ancients of old, yet not repenting to Him so they may be healed. Yes, do not resemble that generation. Who doubting Him, even though they seen Him doing things which were never before seen from the beginning of time until that day. God was in the flesh, in Jesus Christ, appearing to the nation of Israel, making Himself known for the sake of their forefathers, but they did not believe Him just as their forefathers did not believe in the wilderness; however, a select few did receive Him by faith. Although seeing God in the flesh, it was to no avail, because they did not believe Him. He was hidden in the vessel of the likeness of human flesh, and He has no stately form or majesty That we should look upon Him, Nor appearance that we should be attracted to Him. And although they seen Him, they were not able to recognize Him, even though the Scriptures prophesy about Him; and they are read every Sabbath and He, fulfilling them, was amongst them. So it is no different for us today than it was for them: they needed to receive Jesus by faith and so do we, so that faith would be made possible and the righteousness thereof; as Jesus says, But I tell you the truth, it is to your advantage that I go away; for if I do not go away, the Helper will not come to you; but if I go, I will send Him to you. And He, when He comes, will convict the world concerning sin and righteousness and judgment; concerning sin, because they do not believe in Me; and concerning righteousness, because I go to the Father and you no longer see Me; And all things that are spiritual are not seen. God is Spirit. All those who receive Him must receive Him through spiritual means: which is faith.

Let us see the Lord's words to those who doubted in that generation and let us not resemble them and shrink back in disbelief or fear, but instead let us be strengthen in God's grace moving forward, fearlessly, towards the heavenly prize which is ours in Christ Jesus: eternal life. He reprimanded many because of their little faith, as the examples show, And they came to Him and woke Him, saying, “Save us, Lord; we are perishing!” He said to them, "Why are you afraid, you men of little faith?” Then He got up and rebuked the winds and the sea, and it became perfectly calm. And, But seeing the wind, he became frightened, and beginning to sink, he cried out, “Lord, save me!” Immediately Jesus stretched out His hand and took hold of him, and said to him, “You of little faith, why did you doubt?” And, “Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is a lunatic and is very ill; for he often falls into the fire and often into the water. I brought him to Your disciples, and they could not cure him.” And Jesus answered and said, "You unbelieving and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I put up with you?"
Confusion is the product of being double-minded, from the blending together of falsehood and truth, the world and the Kingdom of God. With being in and out, hot and cold, sight becomes like a drunken man, wavering to and fro, or like a ship tossed about by the waves of the sea. So let us take heed lest we become a waverer ourselves, for the things which happened before-hand provide examples to us to whom the end of the ages have come; as the doubter in the Gospel of Mark said, But if You can do anything, take pity on us and help us!” And Jesus said to him, “‘If You can?’ All things are possible to him who believes.” Immediately the boy’s father cried out and said, “I do believe; help my unbelief.” And His disciples, “Why could we not drive it out?” And He said to them, “Because of the littleness of your faith; for truly I say to you, if you have faith the size of a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move; and nothing will be impossible to you.
Let us not be unbelieving and disobedient as these previous generations were, but instead, let us follow the men who are given to us as examples - who are men of faith (Hebrews 11). For all of them who disbelieved, their bodies laid scattered in the wilderness as a testimony; and not one, besides two, Caleb and Joshua, entered into the promised land of the generation in the wilderness. And after Christ's ministry on earth, having multitudes of followers while He was alive, only 120 were obedient and waited in Jerusalem for power from on High, the promised Holy Spirit from God. Let us not think to ourselves, "Oh, if only I lived in that day, I wouldn't be like them!" For, If they do not listen to Moses and the Prophets, they will not be persuaded even if someone rises from the dead. But let us fear instead, lest we ourselves may be found with an unbelieving and wicked heart as they had and persihed. For although having eyes, they did not see; and ears, they did not hear. Generations come and generations go, but all repeat themselves. However, we will all come back again by the resurrection of the righteous and the wicked for everlasting judgement, to receive the recompense of our deeds which were done in the body, and God has elected Christ to sit on His judgement seat, to judge the nations in righteousness. Therefore, we know the terror of the Lord and try to convince others; for the fear of the LORD is the beginning of wisdom. And the root of all disobedience and sin is unbelief.

So let us see for ourselves with the confidence which comes from faith in Jesus Christ - He died, was buried, was raised, and now lives, seated at the right hand of the Father in heaven. You may say, "Good for Him, what does this have to do with me?" It was for your sins that He died, granting the forgiveness of sins thereof, to those who turn to Him through faith; and also the deliverence from sin through His broken body, to be grafted to Himself by faith in the everlasting Word of God. That you may be declared righteous, by a law of faith, through the knowledge of the goodness of God towards you, through His loving-compassion and mercy, through His beloved Son, Jesus Christ, who reconciles you to God by taking upon Himself wrath so that you would serve God in the newness of Spirit and life, not the oldness of the letter of the law of death - for the law condemns, but the Spirit gives life (and these words written to you are Spirit and truth). No longer is it said, "How does the Lord love me?" For He has proven His love toward us in that, While we were still helpless, at the right time Christ died for the ungodly. And to all of us - Jew and Gentile, male and female, slave and free - from the beginning of time to the end, we are brought near to God through Jesus Christ. We are saved by grace.

And this is grace and the truth: seeing the depravity of mankind, God did not send forth a torrent of fire to devour us, but instead, He sent forth His beloved Son, the Son whom He loves and favors, He sent Him forth not to judge the world, but to save it. Although seeing the unseen nature of all of us, He sent forth His Son, on whom abides the great love of God, to die for our sins - who is worthy for such a thing? He knew no sin, but took upon Himself our sins so that we may walk in the righteousness of God. All who are far away are brought near to Him through One; for it says, And there is salvation in no one else; for there is no other name under heaven that has been given among men by which we must be saved. He did this, not while we deserved it, but while we least deserved it, while we were deserving of wrath, He gave us grace. He loved us in the greatest way possible while we were deserving of great wrath as sinners - He knows our transgressions even while they are hidden, yet Jesus died for us openly, and that, to free us from sin and death. Nothing is hidden from His sight and He, seeing our hidden faults, sent His Son into the world to reveal the message of His grace - which is not by works, but is to be received through faith. No-one knows the totality of our sins, or even our thoughts, but God knows; yet, He did not smite us for the greatness of our sins in His sight, but smote His beloved Son, as the Rock in the wilderness (Exodus 17:6) - the punishing of Him brings us peace with God our Father. (He being pure became dirty to make us clean) He takes away the sin of the world by bearing it upon Himself; as John, the Baptist, says, The next day John saw Jesus coming toward him, and said, “Behold! The Lamb of God who takes away the sin of the world! And the apostle Paul, For the love of Christ controls us, having concluded this, that one died for all, therefore all died; And, For the grace of God has appeared, bringing salvation to all men, And God raised Him from the dead, for it was impossible for death to hold Him - He was raised for our justificaiton, by His life we are saved.

Let us rejoice and see the exhortations and exclamations spoken by the Lord Himself to those who would hear and believe in Him, producing in themselves a very great faith, having themselves marveled the Lord by their faith, and called to Him for deliverance because they believed He is powerful and able to do. For these, not being of Hebrew descent, were Gentiles and marveled the Lord; as the examples are found in the Scriptures say, “Lord, my servant is lying paralyzed at home, fearfully tormented.” Jesus said to him, “I will come and heal him.” But the centurion said, “Lord, I am not worthy for You to come under my roof, but just say the word, and my servant will be healed. Now when Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those who were following, “Truly I say to you, I have not found such great faith with anyone in Israel. It is through faith we are saved, by the grace of the Lord Jesus - not starting with faith, then trying to be perfected by the power of human flesh. No! It is from faith to faith, from start to finish, that of faith. If He healed many because of their faith, saying to them, Your faith has saved you; go in peace. And many other such things as these - Luke 5:12; Luke 8:48; Luke 17:19; Luke 18:42 - then how can anyone say otherwise? For in the Gospel the righteousness of God is revealed, stating, For in it the righteousness of God is revealed from faith to faith; as it is written, "BUT THE RIGHTEOUS man SHALL LIVE BY FAITH." And what is sufficient is not a faith that is grand, but a faith the size of a mustard seed. If Christ reprimanded some because of their little faith, then how little that faith must have been! Let us consider the prophet Elijah who is a human like us: he prayed that it would not rain and it did not rain for three and a half years. Then he prayed again for rain, and it rained just as he asked. And the goal of faith? But the goal of our instruction is love from a pure heart and a good conscience and a sincere faith. And love fulfills the entirety of the law, for love does no wrong to a neighbor.

When we believe the Word of God, we correct ourselves to it, setting our seal to this: God is true. And this is the way of life: heeding to the Word of God, correcting ourselves to Him. The one who disbelieves the warning, goes astray; the one who hears and fails to practice what he hears, does likewise. As Wisdom says, For the commandment is a lamp and the teaching is light; And reproofs for discipline are the way of life And, He who keeps instruction is in the way of life, But he who refuses correction goes astray. So it's holding fast to God's Word which saves by the resurrection of Christ; hearing does not make us right with God, but believing. Faith has action. If we believe the Word, it will change us - in faith, repentance lives; as it says, Well then, God has granted to the Gentiles also the repentance that leads to life. Through faith, we gain knowledge of God and in Him, there is righteousness. No-one is righteous except God; as it says, Why do you call Me good? there is none good but one, that is, God. So if anyone be moral, it's because of the morality that is in God through abiding in Christ, as Christ abides in God. No-one has seen God at anytime, but Jesus Christ has made Him known to us - He is the visible image of the invisble God. Through Christ, we can see the Father, imitating Him, not making for ourselves a righteousness of our own through the law, but that which comes through faith in Jesus Christ; and if it's not by works, but by faith, then where is the boasting? We correct ourselves to the Word through rebuke, reproof and discipline. If the Father considers us His own, He will chasten us; also, we are not lacking in the correction from God's family. For this is walking in love: reproving your neighbor (Leviticus 19:17). The wise listen to correction, but a fool departs from it - a fool does not heed to the Word, closing his eyes; he delights in wickedness instead. For in the teaching is life; as it says, Pay close attention to yourself and to your teaching; persevere in these things, for as you do this you will ensure salvation both for yourself and for those who hear you. The Scriptures make one wise unto salvation, and through wisdom, we grow in the grace of the Lord; but a fool does not fear the Lord, but says in His heart, "No God"; and he departs from the way of life. In faith and through faith, we chose the fear of the Lord unto wisdom unto greater grace unto salvation; as it says, you have known the Holy Scriptures, which are able to make you wise for salvation through faith which is in Christ Jesus. Therefore, we ought to love the truth unto salvation; for in vain it does not say, because they did not receive the love of the truth so as to be saved.
By calling upon His name, crying out to Him - with a faith that confesses, that believes, that searches and seeks for the living God despite not being able to see Him - we are saved in hope, and a hope that is seen is not hope at all, for who hopes in that which he sees? We hope in the salvation of God: although we sleep, we will not perish; although we stumble, we do not fall; and it says, WHOEVER WILL CALL ON THE NAME OF THE LORD WILL BE SAVED.
