The Good News
Using bookmarks as tracts has a two-fold purpose: it points to the Word of God for the truth (it is a book marker!) and gives the Gospel, a continual reminder of the Good News. I also use gospel cards because people put them in their wallet; however the best place for all these things is in the heart, meditating and ruminating on them day and night: the paper fades away as a flower of the field, but the Word of God remains forever. I hope these help you with creating your own tracts! There is an offline copy, physical and audio, of the good news which I wrote. I also give out audio bibles in KJV and ESV. Contact me with your name if you want any of these and I will provide them for you to the glory and praise of God, the Father.
