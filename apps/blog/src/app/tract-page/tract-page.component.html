Using bookmarks as tracts has a two-fold purpose: it points to the Word of God for reading and gives the Gospel, offering a continual reminder of the Good News. You can print these off with the double-sided setting and cut along the dotted lines and then laminate them. I hope this helps even with creating your own tracts.
