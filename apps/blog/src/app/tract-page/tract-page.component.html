Tracts

Using bookmarks as tracts has a two-fold purpose: it points to the Word of God for truth and gives the Gospel, offering a continual reminder of the Good News. You can print these off with the double-sided setting and cut along the dotted lines and then laminate them. I hope this helps even with creating your own tracts. There is also free gospel tracts at Fellowship Tract League. I also like using cards because people can put them in their wallet; the best place for all these things is your heart, meditating and ruminating on them day and night: the paper will fade as a flower passes away, but the Word of God remains forever. Amen.
