We eat food, but we are not content; we drink but we are not forever sustained; we eat but we are hungry again later; we drink and are thirsty again later. No matter how much the eye sees, it is not enough; no matter how much the ear hears, it is not content. If these kinds of pleasures truly makes us happy, why don't we express it outwardly? If we were truly happy, shouldn't our happiness resemble a child's happiness? Children are overcome with laughter and express joyfulness and run around expressing their happiness. If these things truly make us happy, why don't we show it? Most often we are chained by such things, and we look miserable outwardly but inwardly, we become less miserable than we were before because our pain has been masked for a little while. This leads to a cycle of dependence - we need those things to keep us in balance. I've been chained by many of these things for years of my life. In a computer, there are entire simulations that are created and essentially allow people to live a new life inside of it. The Danger - Sin

We want to do good in our minds, but in our bodies we don't do that which is good. We don't do the good that we intend to do. Why is that? The Scriptures say, "The spirit is willing, but the flesh is weak". We also have a foe living within us which is sin. We see this every year: we make a new year's resolution and say, 'This year will be nothing like the last, I am going to do this and that!' And we do 'this' and 'that' very well for the first few months, but the time comes when we inevitably go back to our old ways. So you see, in our minds we want to do good, but in our flesh we do not do the good we want; we have good intentions, but the power of our sinful desires carry us away and lead us astray. If we did everything we wanted to do every new year, we would be made more perfect as time goes on; but, in fact, as time goes on, the more we sin and the more we store up in our account transgressions. We are all in a great debt and God's anger burns against our sin, for He Himself is a consuming fire. Our sin needs to be punished because God is just and He cannot let sin go unpunished. But you might say, "Sin in me causes me to do wrong, but I myself want to do good. How is God just in punishing me?". Sin is inseperable from the one who commits sin because the sinner allows sin to be committed and is led astray by sinful desires. Our sin should bring us sorrow, just as someone sinning against us makes us sorrowful. But all sin is against God because sin is transgression against God's Law. New Heavens and New Earth

For behold, I will create new heavens and a new earth. The former things will not be remembered, nor will they come to mind. But be glad and rejoice forever in what I create; for I will create Jerusalem to be a joy and its people to be a delight. I will rejoice in Jerusalem and take delight in My people. The sounds of weeping and crying will no longer be heard in her.
Then I saw a new heaven and a new earth, for the first heaven and earth had passed away, and the sea was no more. saw the holy city, the new Jerusalem, coming down out of heaven from God, prepared as a bride adorned for her husband. And I heard a loud voice from the throne saying: “Behold, the dwelling place of God is with man, and He will dwell with them. They will be His people, and God Himself will be with them as their God. ‘He will wipe away every tear from their eyes,’ and there will be no more death or mourning or crying or pain, for the former things have passed away.” And the One seated on the throne said, “Behold, I make all things new.” Then He said, “Write this down, for these words are faithful and true.”
We have been created new in Christ Jesus if He indeed has given us His Spirit & cut away our old nature with a circumcision not done by human hands. Only the new creation will inherit the kingdom of God; flesh and blood cannot enter the kingdom of God. In Genesis, God created the present world and humanity and all other flesh in 6 days; He will also create the new heavens and new earth which He promised, making all things new, as a place where righteousness dwells; for this present heavens and earth are corrupt and everything rots and decays with use over time because of the dysfunction of the fallen human nature and the death & destruction that comes from it; but the new heavens and new earth will be a place that does not decay nor wear out like a garment, a place where righteousness dwells. For just as God did away with the ancient world with a flood and afterward only Noah and his family (who were righteous) survived on the earth, so will it be when God floods the world again with fire, preserving the righteous who will inherit the new earth: God is preparing a place for us. The first of the new creation is Jesus Christ, Who is the firstfruits of the resurrection, and firstfruits of the new creation; this new creation is not born from will of man, but born of the Spirit. For everything of the old is passing away and only the work of the new will remain; we who are created anew in Christ Jesus will inherit all these things if we persevere until the end. In the beginning, God created everything that we can see; and His work that we do not see is being reenacted in us as it was in the beginning. As it is written, For God, who said, “Light shall shine out of darkness,” is the One who has shone in our hearts to give the Light of the knowledge of the glory of God in the face of Christ.
