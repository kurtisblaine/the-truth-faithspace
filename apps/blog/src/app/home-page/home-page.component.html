The Good News of The Kingdom of God
The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a king who gave a wedding feast for his son, and sent his servants to call those who were invited to the wedding feast, but they would not come. Again he sent other servants, saying, ‘Tell those who are invited, “See, I have prepared my dinner, my oxen and my fat calves have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding feast.”’ But they paid no attention and went off, one to his farm, another to his business, while the rest seized his servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them. The king was angry, and he sent his troops and destroyed those murderers and burned their city. Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding feast is ready, but those invited were not worthy. Go therefore to the main roads and invite to the wedding feast as many as you find.’ And those servants went out into the roads and gathered all whom they found, both bad and good. So the wedding hall was filled with guests. But when the king came in to look at the guests, he saw there a man who had no wedding garment. And he said to him, Friend, how did you get in here without a wedding garment?’ And he was speechless. Then the king said to the attendants, ‘Bind him hand and foot and cast him into the outer darkness. In that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ For many are called, but few are chosen.
He had no form or majesty that we should look at Him, and no beauty that we should desire Him. He was despised and rejected by men, a man of sorrows, and acquainted with grief; and as one from whom men hide their faces He was despised, and we esteemed Him not. Surely He has borne our griefs and carried our sorrows; yet we esteemed Him stricken, smitten by God, and afflicted. But He was pierced for our transgressions; He was crushed for our iniquities; upon Him was the chastisement that brought us peace, and with His wounds we are healed. All we like sheep have gone astray; we have turned—every one—to his own way; and the LORD has laid on Him the iniquity of us all. He was oppressed, and He was afflicted, yet He opened not His mouth; like a lamb that is led to the slaughter, and like a sheep that before its shearers is silent, so He opened not his mouth.
By oppression and judgment He was taken away; and as for His generation, who considered that He was cut off out of the land of the living, stricken for the transgression of My people? And they made His grave with the wicked and with a rich man in His death, although He had done no violence, and there was no deceit in His mouth. Yet it was the will of the LORD to crush Him; He has put Him to grief; when His soul makes an offering for guilt, He shall see his offspring; He shall prolong His days; the will of the LORD shall prosper in His hand. Out of the anguish of His soul he shall see and be satisfied; by His knowledge shall the righteous One, My Servant, make many to be accounted righteous, and He shall bear their iniquities. Therefore I will divide Him a portion with the many, and He shall divide the spoil with the strong, because He poured out His soul to death and was numbered with the transgressors; yet He bore the sin of many, and makes intercession for the transgressors.
Moses writes this about the righteousness that is by the law: “The person who does these things will live by them.” But the righteousness that is by faith says: “Do not say in your heart, ‘Who will ascend into heaven?’ ” (that is, to bring Christ down) “or ‘Who will descend into the deep?’ ” (that is, to bring Christ up from the dead). But what does it say? “The word is near you; it is in your mouth and in your heart,” that is, the message concerning faith that we proclaim: If you declare with your mouth, “Jesus is Lord,” and believe in your heart that God raised him from the dead, you will be saved. For it is with your heart that you believe and are justified, and it is with your mouth that you profess your faith and are saved. As Scripture says, “Anyone who believes in him will never be put to shame.” For there is no difference between Jew and Gentile — the same Lord is Lord of all and richly blesses all who call on him, for, 'Everyone who calls on the name of the Lord will be saved.'
For the Word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart. And no creature is hidden from his sight, but all are naked and exposed to the eyes of him to whom we must give account.
I appeal to you therefore, brothers, by the mercies of God, to present your bodies as a living sacrifice, holy and acceptable to God, which is your spiritual worship. Do not be conformed to this world, but be transformed by the renewal of your mind, that by testing you may discern what is the will of God, what is good and acceptable and perfect.
‘Love the Lord your God with all your heart and with all your soul and with all your mind.’ This is the first and greatest commandment. And the second is like it: ‘Love your neighbor as yourself.’ All the Law and the Prophets hang on these two commandments.

Acknowledgment

We don't know where we came from; we can't say what we did a day ago. We don't know where we are going; we can't say what will happen tomorrow. All we know is today; all we know is right now. Today, is the day of salvation; right now, is the time of God's favor. You will hear His voice, do not harden your hearts.

The Danger - Futility

Pleasure

Alcohol, drugs, food and all other pleasures do not sustain us. They make us happy for a time, but they do not make us happy forever. All the pleasures of the world would not be enough. We eat and we are not content; we drink and we are not sustained. We eat and are hungry again; we drink and are thirsty again. No matter how much the eye sees, it is not enough; No matter how much the ear hears, it is not content. If this kind of pleasure truly makes us happy, why don't we express it? If we are truly happy, shouldn't our happiness resemble a child's happiness? Children are overcome with laughter and express joyfulness and run around expressing their happiness. If these things truly make us happy, why don't we show it? All things are full of weariness; a man cannot utter it; the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing. What has been is what will be, and what has been done is what will be done, and there is nothing new under the sun. Is there a thing of which it is said, “See, this is new”? It has been already in the ages before us. There is no remembrance of former things, nor will there be any remembrance of later things yet to be among those who come after. We live in an age where media is everywhere. It started with televisions in the living room then escalated to monitors in our pockets. Is this a bad thing? The human eye consists of 3 cones responsible for color vision: red, green and blue; in the same way, these screens are made up of a pixels with these 3 colors. This is why only humans can see a computer screen or television screen as intended -- it minics the human eye. So, man learns how God creates things then mimics them as with television screens. Therefore, while we look through our eyes, we look through the eyes of a screen. Whatever another person wants you to see, you will see. Blessed are your eyes for seeing this! You now see that these eyes can be used for good or evil. Therefore, be watchful as you look from your eyes, knowing the things we hear and see enter into our hearts. I said in my heart, “Come now, I will test you with pleasure; enjoy yourself.” But behold, this also was vanity. I said of laughter, “It is mad,” and of pleasure, “What use is it?” I searched with my heart how to cheer my body with wine—my heart still guiding me with wisdom—and how to lay hold on folly, till I might see what was good for the children of man to do under heaven during the few days of their life. (...) And whatever my eyes desired I did not keep from them. I kept my heart from no pleasure, for my heart found pleasure in all my toil, and this was my reward for all my toil. Then I considered all that my hands had done and the toil I had expended in doing it, and behold, all was vanity and a striving after wind, and there was nothing to be gained under the sun.

Toil

Our lives are filled with work. We work and sleep just to do it all over again. And for what? We recieve wages for our toil, but we have no time to spend the money we have earned. Even then, our money is given right back in the form of bills and taxes. Our days are short yet we spend them in pain and agony from our much work. We work for things that will not matter in the future; we work for objectives that will not matter years from now. In the future, people will look back and won't care what we are currently doing. Yet, we strive and labor and don't recieve our due wages. Instead, the boss does little work and eats the fruits of our labor. What is the best use of time in our short lives? I hated all my toil in which I toil under the sun, seeing that I must leave it to the man who will come after me, and who knows whether he will be wise or a fool? Yet he will be master of all for which I toiled and used my wisdom under the sun. This also is vanity. So I turned about and gave my heart up to despair over all the toil of my labors under the sun, because sometimes a person who has toiled with wisdom and knowledge and skill must leave everything to be enjoyed by someone who did not toil for it. This also is vanity and a great evil. What has a man from all the toil and striving of heart with which he toils beneath the sun? For all his days are full of sorrow, and his work is a vexation. Even in the night his heart does not rest. This also is vanity. Life is short and we spend our lives working for other people's objectives; their will is to get rich and spend their lives in luxury. What then? Should we not work? Of course not! Do you think a doctor enjoys an abundant life thinking everything is so serious? He goes in and out of surgery, working hard all the days of his life, but hardly has time to enjoy his family. His surgeries are meticulous; his hands are steady; his mind is clear. The worse case scenario is death; the best case scenario is a life saved. If a doctor is so steady handed in these cases, why do we think so seriously of our jobs?

Wealth

The futility of life and wealth is like a man who goes into a pub with everything he owns. He sits down at a gambling table to roll dice. At first, he bets only a little bit of what he owns. He rolls the dice and a miracle happens! All the dice line up. The man yells, 'Today is my lucky day. I will bet more!' So he rolls again but with more of his possessions on the table. A miracle happens again! The dice all line up. The man continues this over and over many times; eventually, he has everything he owns on the table even the shirt off his back! He thinks to himself, 'Surely I have rolled these dice over and over and they haven't failed me!' However, the man handing out the dice sees the man and thinks, 'You fool! One day these dice will not line up and everything you have will be mine. For I have worked a long time in this pub and I see it happen over and over.' Then the day came when the dice did not line up and everything on the table was taken from the man. Because the man didn't even have enough money for a beer, he was then cast out of the pub into darkenss where he wept. He who loves money will not be satisfied with money, nor he who loves wealth with his income; this also is vanity. When goods increase, they increase who eat them, and what advantage has their owner but to see them with his eyes? Sweet is the sleep of a laborer, whether he eats little or much, but the full stomach of the rich will not let him sleep. There is a grievous evil that I have seen under the sun: riches were kept by their owner to his hurt, and those riches were lost in a bad venture. And he is father of a son, but he has nothing in his hand. As he came from his mother's womb he shall go again, naked as he came, and shall take nothing for his toil that he may carry away in his hand. This also is a grievous evil: just as he came, so shall he go, and what gain is there to him who toils for the wind? Moreover, all his days he eats in darkness in much vexation and sickness and anger. Look around! Point to something that you can take with you when you leave this earth; not even your own body will go with you. In the beginning, we were created from the dust of the earth; in the end, we will return to dust. In the beginning, our spirits were breathed into our bodies and we became living souls; in the end, our spirits will return to God who gave them. When we return to Him, it will be as if only a moment passed. Train yourself for godliness; for while bodily training is of some value, godliness is of value in every way, as it holds promise for the present life and also for the life to come.

Death

This present life is like a man who lives in a tent for a little while. He is in anguish in his tent. The man hopes and looks forward to his permenant home. Then, the day came when his tent was taken down with the rushing of a wind and a violent storm. The tent was destroyed and the man went to his permenant home where he spent the rest of his days forever and ever. I concluded that God is testing us, to show us that we are no better than animals. After all, the same fate awaits human beings and animals alike. One dies just like the other. They are both the same kind of creature. A human being is no better off than an animal, because life has no meaning for either. They are both going to the same place — the dust. They both came from it; they will both go back to it. How can anyone be sure that the human spirit goes upwards while an animal's spirit goes down into the ground? Also, the hearts of the children of man are full of evil, and madness is in their hearts while they live, and after that they go to the dead. But he who is joined with all the living has hope, for a living dog is better than a dead lion. For the living know that they will die, but the dead know nothing, and they have no more reward, for the memory of them is forgotten. Their love and their hate and their envy have already perished, and forever they have no more share in all that is done under the sun. (...) Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might, for there is no work or thought or knowledge or wisdom in Sheol (the place of the dead), to which you are going. We only have one life to live; no one asked to be born; no one chose their family; no one chose the body they would have; no one chose what country they would be born into. We are given what we have; we have been delt cards that cannot be taken back. God formed all of us with a purpose for a time and place as this. Woe to him who strives with Him who formed him, a pot among earthen pots! Does the clay say to him who forms it, ‘What are you making?’ or ‘Your work has no handles’? Woe to him who says to a father, ‘What are you begetting?’ or to a woman, ‘With what are you in labor?’

The Danger - Sin

Consuming Fire

God is for us and not against us. He is Love and He is Good. Not a bad thought crosses His mind. It is impossible for Him to tell a lie. He loves truth and justice and righteousness, but hates evil and sin and wrong. He is Light and there is no darkness in Him at all. He does not share His glory with another and He never changes. He is now, was from the beginning, and is yet to come in the future.

God is three and one: the Will of God or the Father, the Word of God or Jesus Christ, and the Power of God or the Holy Spirit.

God is forever and His love is unfailing. Heaven is His throne and the earth is His footstool and His Spirit chooses to live in human hearts. God is everywhere and knows everything. He is the First and the Last, He has no beginning nor end. God is not taught good, He is good. God is not taught love, He is love. God was not taught anything and everything comes from Him -- the good and the bad. God is a consuming fire and His very name is Jealous; He is jealous of anything we put in His place in our life.

If a husband loves his wife and his wife cheats on him with another man, the husband is going to feel two very strong emotions: anger and jealousy. Are the emotions in this scenario evil? No. They are powerful emotions that come from the husband's love for his wife. If the husband did not love his wife, then he would not feel anger or jealousy. Also, if a father has a child and the child continually plays with dangerous things like snakes and knives, the father will discipline the child and start to hate the snakes and knives which cause his child harm. Is the discipline done because the father hates the child? Of course not! In fact, the discipline is proof that the father loves his child and it's a sign that the father considers the child his own. If the father did not discipline the child, that would mean the child is illegitimate and a bastard. The father's hatred is directed towards the things that cause his child danger because he loves him. Everything is God's and He is good all the time; His love does not fail.

He Sees and Hears

God is living and sees the thoughts and intentions of every human heart, even when we ourselves do not know our own motives or intentions. We may think in our hearts, 'My motives are right. I will stand before God and He will agree.' Are our thoughts, God's thoughts? Are our ways, God's ways? As the heavens are higher than the earth, so are God's ways higher than your ways and His thoughts higher than your thoughts. I do not even judge myself. For I am not aware of anything against myself, but I am not thereby acquitted. It is the Lord who judges me. Therefore do not pronounce judgment before the time, before the Lord comes, who will bring to light the things now hidden in darkness and will disclose the purposes of the heart. Do we think we can hide from God? Is there any place on earth where He isn't? Isn't His eyes everywhere? In God's throne room, His mighty Cheribum are covered with eyes. Not eyes that are blind; but eyes that can see. Not eyes like we humans have, not eyes like an eagle which can spot a worm from miles away -- no. God seen what was happening below on Mount Sinai. God seen where Jonah went to get away from God. If that is not enough, He also sends His Watchers, which are mighty angels to look after us. He who planted the ear, does he not hear? He who formed the eye, does he not see? He who disciplines the nations, does he not rebuke? He who teaches man knowledge — the LORD — knows the thoughts of man, that they are but a breath. The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. And the LORD regretted that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart.

Our Sin Nature

None is righteous, no, not one; no one understands; no one seeks for God. All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one. Their throat is an open grave; they use their tongues to deceive. The venom of asps is under their lips. Their mouth is full of curses and bitterness. Their feet are swift to shed blood; in their paths are ruin and misery, and the way of peace they have not known. There is no fear of God before their eyes. A new born baby is precious; but, is the baby good? If we are good, then why do we need to be taught good? If we were good, then we wouldn't need to be taught good; but it is true that we inheritly know bad; we inherit this 'knowing' from the first man, Adam. In the garden, he sinned against God and was decieved by Eve; so, since one man sinned once, all became sinners. As a child we are taught good and we already know evil - that which is evil we don't need to learn, but that which is good we need to learn. This is our nature; we don't need convincing; it's effortless for us to do bad and takes all our energy to do good. As we grow old and we are not taught good, our parents are blamed. But why? People say, 'I blame the parents for the child's behavior' or 'Why didn't the parents teach them what is right?'. Therefore, we testify to ourselves about the way we are. Even more we say, 'I am only human' when we do wrong. This confirms to ourselves and others that we belong to a nature that is fallen and broken. It is our nature as it's an eagle's nature to fly. The trouble is not with the law, for it is spiritual and good. The trouble is with me, for I am all too human, a slave to sin. I don’t really understand myself, for I want to do what is right, but I don’t do it. Instead, I do what I hate. But if I know that what I am doing is wrong, this shows that I agree that the law is good. So I am not the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. And I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my sinful nature. I want to do what is right, but I can’t. I want to do what is good, but I don’t. I don’t want to do what is wrong, but I do it anyway. But if I do what I don’t want to do, I am not really the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. I have discovered this principle of life-—that when I want to do what is right, I inevitably do what is wrong. I love God’s law with all my heart. But there is another power within me that is at war with my mind. This power makes me a slave to the sin that is still within me. Oh, what a miserable person I am! Who will free us from this life that is dominated by sin and death? Thank God! The answer is in Jesus Christ our Lord.

The Day of Judgement

Everything done in our body will need to be accounted for on Judgement Day. This means every Work and every Word will need to be accounted for. God is the Just Judge and He is so just that He cannot let anyone go unpunished. If He let just one go unpunished, He wouldn't be Good and Just. People in this world go and riot when justice is not upheld. When someone who is supposed to be punished for their crimes gets off free, they go out and riot against the judge and police and say, 'You are no good! Off with your head!' God is not like any judge on earth. He will punish all evil. I will discipline you, but with justice; I cannot let you go unpunished. Therefore, since everyone of us is sinful and God sees and hears everything that everyone does and God will not let anyone go unpunished -- Who then can be saved? Aren't we all doomed? If there is no hope, then, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die". Isn't the danger so evident? What must we do to be saved from the wrath of God? What must we do to be free'd from the power of sin and death? (The command) is not in heaven, that you should say, ‘Who will ascend to heaven for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?’ Neither is it beyond the sea, that you should say, ‘Who will go over the sea for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?’ But the word is very near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it. The world thinks that the truth is hidden: in the depths of the ocean, in outer space, or in the center of the earth. They spend countless thousands of dollars to explore where no man has gone before. They build towers up into heaven to reach God just as they did in times past at the Tower of Babel. They do not know, but you now know: The Truth has came and the Truth has left and the Truth sent His Spirit and the Truth will come again. Who will free us from this life that is dominated by sin and death? Thank God! The answer is in Jesus Christ our Lord.

The Call - Grace

Jesus, the Suffering Servant, the Promised Messiah

You have heard it before, but did you understand it? Jesus died on the Cross for the sin of the world. When we deserved punishment and wrath from God, He instead gave us His Son, born of a women and in likeness of human flesh, like we sinners have, to put to death sin in the flesh. He has all authority and power over the spiritual realm; all the demonic forces that wage war against us, Jesus defeated on the Cross and then gave the authority to us. When He was put on the Cross to die, He was made a public spectacle of by the world; but through the Cross, He made a public spectacle of the powers and authorities that govern this world.

Through Jesus' shed Blood and broken body, God punished us; Jesus Christ took the punishment that we deserve to free us from sin. God's law requires that we die for our sins; But Jesus Christ died in our place. Through God's holy law, we stored up God's wrath for the day of judgement; But through Jesus Christ, we have all been freely forgiven of all sin -- past, present, and future. We have all been called according to God's wonderful grace! Therefore no one can boast in the presence of God or in the presence of men since we have all been made right with God by grace through our Lord, the suffering Servant; if we do boast, we boast about our Lord and Savior, Jesus Christ who gave up His life for us because of His great love for us.

Too good to be true? Well now, you are beginning to know God for He is Good and He is Truth. No sin can now prevent us from crying out to God. All have been called through the precious Blood of Christ. His Blood now cries out for forgiveness instead of vengeance like the blood of Abel. Nothing now can seperate us from the love of God that is in Christ Jesus our Lord. Hallelijah!

Jesus, the Rock

They said, “Why did you bring us up out of Egypt to make us and our children and livestock die of thirst?” Then Moses cried out to the Lord, “What am I to do with these people? They are almost ready to stone me.” The Lord answered Moses, “Go out in front of the people. Take with you some of the elders of Israel and take in your hand the staff with which you struck the Nile, and go. I will stand there before you by the rock at Horeb. Strike the rock, and water will come out of it for the people to drink.” So Moses did this in the sight of the elders of Israel. The people had stony hearts that were bent on wickedness; they were just rescued from Egypt and they wanted to stone Moses; Moses is like Jesus for us today, the mediator between the people and God. So, in their angery stony hearts, they wanted to share the contents of their hearts with physical stones to kill Moses; the anger in their stony hearts was expressed with actual stones. Moses is the mediator for God, He speaks on behalf of God and the people's anger was director towards God, not Moses; so actually the people were angry with God. God could have gave the people what they deserved but instead of striking the rock of the people's hearts and wounding them, He struck a different Rock, Jesus, and out came living water for the people to drink. A spiritual drink that is not like a physical drink; a drink makes you never thirst again. Water doesn't come from rocks, but from springs and Jesus didn't come from earth, but from heaven. “Then are you also without understanding? Do you not see that whatever goes into a person from outside cannot defile him, since it enters not his heart but his stomach, and is expelled?” (Thus he declared all foods clean.) And he said, “What comes out of a person is what defiles him. For from within, out of the heart of man, come evil thoughts, sexual immorality, theft, murder, adultery, coveting, wickedness, deceit, sensuality, envy, slander, pride, foolishness. All these evil things come from within, and they defile a person." Moses then struck the rock again another time when God told him to speak to the Rock to bring forth living water. Moses disobeyed and struck the rock again twice; he was then banished from entering into the promise land. This serves as a warning for us today. Christ was sacrificed once and for all, not many times. If we deliberately keep on sinning after we have received the knowledge of the truth, no sacrifice for sins is left, but only a fearful expectation of judgment and of raging fire that will consume the enemies of God. If we struggle with sin, let us talk to our Rock and get deliverence from our sin, instead of striking Him again. For we were the ones who killed Jesus, He bore our sins.

Jesus, the Lamb

Under the Old Covenant, people were forgiven by God for their sins by sacrificing an animal. When we sin, something's blood has to be shed; since with sin comes death; these two go hand-in-hand, just as righteousness and life are friends. Whoever sows to please their flesh, from the flesh will reap destruction; whoever sows to please the Spirit, from the Spirit will reap eternal life. In fact, the law requires that nearly everything be cleansed with blood, and without the shedding of blood there is no forgiveness. So the Israelites had to sacrifice a lamb to be forgiven of their sin. Likewise, Jesus was sacrificed today for the forgiveness of our sins; not a sacrifice that needed to be offered over and over, but once and for all.

When the Israelites were rescued out from Egypt, they had to sacrifice another lamb and paint their doorpost with its blood. The angel of death then came in through the city and slayed everyone whose doorpost was not marked with blood; the angel passed over their house and did not harm anyone inside. This is now remembered today as the Passover. In the same way, Jesus was sacrificed and we have been washed clean of sin by His Blood; we bear His Blood on us and wear Christ like new clothes as the old clothes of sin have been pinned to the Cross. The second death now has no power over us who have put our trust in Jesus; the second death is the lake of fire, where people die after they die.

Jesus, the Door

The world was judged before by water; you may have heard about Noah and the arc in Genesis. God was sorry that He ever created humans because of their great wickedness; He warned Noah to build an arc and to tell the people to get inside because God is going to send a flood. In the beginning, water came from springs out of the earth, not from rain in the sky; so when Noah told them about this, the people ignored him and thought he was crazy. Flood waters did come from the sky and God released the water from inside the earth to flood all of the land. But everyone who entered in through the door of the arc were saved.

Likewise today, whoever enters in through the Door who is Jesus Christ will be saved from the judgement of the earth, not by water, but by fire. We are saved from the world and its desires that are detestable in the sight of God. Who do we fool? If we say we are saved from sin but continue in it, are we truly saved? If we say we are saved from the world but stay friends with the world, are we truly saved? You adulterous people, donʼt you know that friendship with the world means enmity against God? Therefore, anyone who chooses to be a friend of the world becomes an enemy of God. I have come to bring fire on the earth, and how I wish it were already kindled! The world as we know it is passing away and anyone of it will be found deserving judgement. But greater is the Spirit in us than the spirit of the world; the Spirit in us is the Spirit of Jesus and He has overcome the world. Just as those who entered into the ark floated above the destruction of the earth, those who have entered in through Jesus will be caught up with Jesus when He returns on the last day during the rapture.

Jesus, the Lion

Then Samson went down with his father and mother to Timnah, and they came to the vineyards of Timnah. And behold, a young lion came toward him roaring. Then the Spirit of the Lord rushed upon him, and although he had nothing in his hand, he tore the lion in pieces as one tears a young goat. But he did not tell his father or his mother what he had done. Then he went down and talked with the woman, and she was right in Samson's eyes. After some days he returned to take her. And he turned aside to see the carcass of the lion, and behold, there was a swarm of bees in the body of the lion, and honey. He scraped it out into his hands and went on, eating as he went. And he came to his father and mother and gave some to them, and they ate. But he did not tell them that he had scraped the honey from the carcass of the lion. (...) 'Out of the eater came something to eat. Out of the strong came something sweet.' A lion is strong and powerful; no one takes a lion and brings it in their home for their children to play with because the lion is powerful. A lion typically isn't sweet -- it won't let you pet it and it won't give you kisses!
