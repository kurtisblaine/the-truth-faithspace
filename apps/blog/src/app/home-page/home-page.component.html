The Good News of The Kingdom of God
The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a king who gave a wedding feast for his son, and sent his servants to call those who were invited to the wedding feast, but they would not come. Again he sent other servants, saying, ‘Tell those who are invited, “See, I have prepared my dinner, my oxen and my fat calves have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding feast.”’ But they paid no attention and went off, one to his farm, another to his business, while the rest seized his servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them. The king was angry, and he sent his troops and destroyed those murderers and burned their city. Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding feast is ready, but those invited were not worthy. Go therefore to the main roads and invite to the wedding feast as many as you find.’ And those servants went out into the roads and gathered all whom they found, both bad and good. So the wedding hall was filled with guests. But when the king came in to look at the guests, he saw there a man who had no wedding garment. And he said to him, Friend, how did you get in here without a wedding garment?’ And he was speechless. Then the king said to the attendants, ‘Bind him hand and foot and cast him into the outer darkness. In that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ For many are called, but few are chosen.

Disclaimer

I cannot speak of these important matters as they ought to be spoken; the Holy Spirit will speak through me. We don't know where we came from; we can't say what we did a day ago. We don't know where we are going; we can't say what will happen tomorrow. All we know is today; all you know is right now. Today, is the day of salvation; right now, is the time of God's favor. When you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts. You know the power of human words. How much more powerful the Word of God? For the Word of God is living and active, sharper than any two-edged sword, piercing to the division of soul and of spirit, of joints and of marrow, and discerning the thoughts and intentions of the heart. And no creature is hidden from his sight, but all are naked and exposed to the eyes of him to whom we must give account.

The Danger - Futility

Self-Indulgence

Alcohol and drugs and food and all other pleasures under the sun do not sustain us. They make us happy for a time, but they do not make us happy forever. All the pleasure in the world would not be enough. We eat and we are not content; we drink and we are not sustained. We eat and are hungry again; we drink and are thirsty again. If this kind of pleasure truly makes us happy, why don't we express it? If we are truly happy, shouldn't our happiness resemble a child's happiness? Children are overcome with laughter and express joyfulness and run around expressing their happiness which can hardly be contained in their little bodies. If these indulgences of ours truly make us happy, why don't we show it? I said in my heart, “Come now, I will test you with pleasure; enjoy yourself.” But behold, this also was vanity. I said of laughter, “It is mad,” and of pleasure, “What use is it?” I searched with my heart how to cheer my body with wine—my heart still guiding me with wisdom—and how to lay hold on folly, till I might see what was good for the children of man to do under heaven during the few days of their life. I made great works. I built houses and planted vineyards for myself. I made myself gardens and parks, and planted in them all kinds of fruit trees. I made myself pools from which to water the forest of growing trees. I bought male and female slaves, and had slaves who were born in my house. I had also great possessions of herds and flocks, more than any who had been before me in Jerusalem. I also gathered for myself silver and gold and the treasure of kings and provinces. I got singers, both men and women, and many concubines, the delight of the sons of man. So I became great and surpassed all who were before me in Jerusalem. Also my wisdom remained with me. And whatever my eyes desired I did not keep from them. I kept my heart from no pleasure, for my heart found pleasure in all my toil, and this was my reward for all my toil. Then I considered all that my hands had done and the toil I had expended in doing it, and behold, all was vanity and a striving after wind, and there was nothing to be gained under the sun.

Toil

I hated all my toil in which I toil under the sun, seeing that I must leave it to the man who will come after me, and who knows whether he will be wise or a fool? Yet he will be master of all for which I toiled and used my wisdom under the sun. This also is vanity. So I turned about and gave my heart up to despair over all the toil of my labors under the sun, because sometimes a person who has toiled with wisdom and knowledge and skill must leave everything to be enjoyed by someone who did not toil for it. This also is vanity and a great evil. What has a man from all the toil and striving of heart with which he toils beneath the sun? For all his days are full of sorrow, and his work is a vexation. Even in the night his heart does not rest. This also is vanity.

Wealth

He who loves money will not be satisfied with money, nor he who loves wealth with his income; this also is vanity. When goods increase, they increase who eat them, and what advantage has their owner but to see them with his eyes? Sweet is the sleep of a laborer, whether he eats little or much, but the full stomach of the rich will not let him sleep. There is a grievous evil that I have seen under the sun: riches were kept by their owner to his hurt, and those riches were lost in a bad venture. And he is father of a son, but he has nothing in his hand. As he came from his mother's womb he shall go again, naked as he came, and shall take nothing for his toil that he may carry away in his hand. This also is a grievous evil: just as he came, so shall he go, and what gain is there to him who toils for the wind? Moreover, all his days he eats in darkness in much vexation and sickness and anger.

The Danger - Sin

He is a Consuming Fire

God is for us and not against us. He is Love and He is Good. Not a bad thought crosses His mind. It is impossible for Him to tell a lie. He loves truth and justice and righteousness, but hates evil and sin and wrong. He is Light and there is no darkness in Him at all. He does not share His glory with another and He never changes. He is now, was from the beginning, and is yet to come in the future. God is three and one: the Will of God or the Father, the Word of God or Jesus Christ, and the Power of God or the Holy Spirit. God is forever and His love is unfailing. Heaven is His throne and the earth is His footstool and His Spirit chooses to live in human hearts. God is everywhere and knows everything. He is the First and the Last, He has no beginning nor end. God is not taught good, He is good. God is not taught love, He is love. God was not taught anything and everything comes from Him -- the good and the bad. God is a consuming fire and His very name is Jealous; He is jealous of anything we put in His place in our life. If a husband loves his wife and his wife cheats on him with another man, the husband is going to feel two very strong emotions: anger and jealousy. Are the emotions in this scenario evil? No. They are powerful emotions that come from the husband's love for his wife. If the husband did not love his wife, then he would not feel anger or jealousy. Also, if a father has a child and the child continually plays with dangerous things like snakes or knives. The father will discipline the child and start to hate the snakes and knives which cause his child harm. Is the discipline done because the father hates the child? Of course not! In fact, the discipline is proof that the father loves his child and it's a sign that the father considers the child his own. If the father did not discipline the child, that would mean the child is illegitimate or a bastard. The father's hatred is directed towards the things that cause his child danger because he loves him. Everything is God's and He is good all the time; His love does not fail.

He Sees and Hears

God is living and sees the thoughts and intentions of every human heart, even when we ourselves do not know our own motives or intentions. We may think in our hearts, 'My motives are right. I will stand before God and He will agree.' Are our thoughts, God's thoughts? Are our ways, God's ways? As the heavens are higher than the earth, so are God's ways higher than your ways and His thoughts higher than your thoughts. I do not even judge myself. For I am not aware of anything against myself, but I am not thereby acquitted. It is the Lord who judges me. Therefore do not pronounce judgment before the time, before the Lord comes, who will bring to light the things now hidden in darkness and will disclose the purposes of the heart. Do we think we can hide from God? Is there any place on earth where He isn't? Isn't His eyes everywhere? In God's throne room, His mighty Cheribum are covered with eyes. Not eyes that are blind; but eyes that can see. Not eyes like we humans have, not eyes like an eagle which can spot a worm from miles away -- no. God seen what was happening below on Mount Sinai. God seen where Jonah went to get away from God. If that is not enough, He also sends His Watchers, which are mighty angels to look after us. He who planted the ear, does he not hear? He who formed the eye, does he not see? He who disciplines the nations, does he not rebuke? He who teaches man knowledge — the LORD — knows the thoughts of man, that they are but a breath. The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. And the LORD regretted that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart.

Our Sin Nature

Does anyone continually do good? Is anyone of us truly seeking God? How many of us do good all the time? Who speaks correctly all the time? Which one of us does the right thing and chooses good rather than evil? None is righteous, no, not one; no one understands; no one seeks for God. All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one. Their throat is an open grave; they use their tongues to deceive. The venom of asps is under their lips. Their mouth is full of curses and bitterness. Their feet are swift to shed blood; in their paths are ruin and misery, and the way of peace they have not known. There is no fear of God before their eyes. A new born baby is precious. But if we are good, then why do we need to be taught good at a young age? We inheritly know evil, but why don't we know good? As a child we are taught good, but we already know evil - that which is evil we don't need to learn. As we grow older and are not taught good, our parents are blamed. People will say, 'I blame the parents for the child's behavior' or 'Why didn't their parents teach them what is right'. There is a sin nature that we have all been born with. People of the world say, 'I am only human' when they do something wrong. This confirms to themselves and others that they have a nature that is fallen and broken. This is the sin nature: The trouble is not with the law, for it is spiritual and good. The trouble is with me, for I am all too human, a slave to sin. I don’t really understand myself, for I want to do what is right, but I don’t do it. Instead, I do what I hate. But if I know that what I am doing is wrong, this shows that I agree that the law is good. So I am not the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. And I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my sinful nature. I want to do what is right, but I can’t. I want to do what is good, but I don’t. I don’t want to do what is wrong, but I do it anyway. But if I do what I don’t want to do, I am not really the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. I have discovered this principle of life-—that when I want to do what is right, I inevitably do what is wrong. I love God’s law with all my heart. But there is another power within me that is at war with my mind. This power makes me a slave to the sin that is still within me. Oh, what a miserable person I am!

The Day of Judgement

Everything done in our body will need to be accounted for on Judgement Day. This means every Work and every Word will need to be accounted for. God is the Just Judge and He is so just that He cannot let anyone go unpunished. If He let just one go unpunished, He wouldn't be Good and Just. People in this world go and riot when justice is not upheld. When someone who is supposed to be punished for their crimes gets off free, they go out and riot against the judge and police and say, 'You are no good! Off with your head!' God is not like any judge on earth. He will punish all evil. I will discipline you, but with justice; I cannot let you go unpunished. Therefore, since everyone of us is sinful and God sees and hears everything that everyone does and God will not let anyone go unpunished -- Who then can be saved? Aren't we all doomed? If there is no hope, then, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die". Isn't the danger so evident? What must we do to be saved from the wrath of God? What must we do to be free'd from the power of sin and death? (The command) is not in heaven, that you should say, ‘Who will ascend to heaven for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?’ Neither is it beyond the sea, that you should say, ‘Who will go over the sea for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?’ But the word is very near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it. The world thinks that the truth is hidden: in the depths of the ocean, in outer space, or in the center of the earth. They spend countless thousands of dollars to explore where no man has gone before. They build towers up into heaven to reach God just as they did in times past at the Tower of Babel. They do not know, but you now know: The Truth has came and the Truth has left and the Truth sent His Spirit and the Truth will come again. Who will free us from this life that is dominated by sin and death? Thank God! The answer is in Jesus Christ our Lord.
