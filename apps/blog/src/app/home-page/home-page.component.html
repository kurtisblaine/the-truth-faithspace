Holy, Holy, Holy is He

My name is Kurtis. Below is the testimony the Lord gave me. Use the navigation in the top left corner. Click here to start reading Scripture. Right before Covid-19, I had just got my full time job, experienced military life and college. What was left but to get married, have kids and die? I said to myself, “Surely life isn’t here, but death, but where?” I started seeking. I figured mankind doesn’t know, if they did, wouldn’t they tell me? But all my life no-one told me the truth. I don't remember ever seeing a street preacher. Nor do I remember anything spectacular in church service. We were the family that went to church during the holidays and once in a while outside of that, but it was always boring to me.

Later on, I seen the churches in America that are claiming God aren’t trying to convince me of God, so they must not really know God; for the one who is convinced, convinces others. I also seen that it was very popular -- on almost every street corner. And I said to myself, “Surely if the truth exists, only few would know it”. I thought that the truth wouldn't be popular. For one: is not the truth a mystery? And two: isn't it painful?



I started to open my Bible. My mom had given it to me. She was on a journey herself with God and gave all her things to the poor. I said “What? This is from God?” (The only other time I remember opening my Bible was when my step dad died and right before leaving for the military). Then she moved out of the state. That made me want to seek all the more. So I opened up my Bible and remembered the change it had on my life; although the change was quick, it wasn’t drastic, I had peace and other spiritual things, but I was still in sin.

I remember doing much work at my new job and reading Scripture. I remember praying to God, “Ah! If only I could seek You much more.” (I don’t know if it was an answer to my prayers or not) But Covid-19 happened and I was put in solitude, working from home. I read. The Scripture that opened my eyes up was Ecclesiastes: I seen the wisdom and the futility of life. Also the Psalms: I had very much conviction and judgement because of my sin and desire for righteousness in God. And Isaiah: the revealing of my own wicked heart and the redemption of God. The Scriptures were very bitter & like death to me because I didn't obey, but I hoped in God. I started at the beginning of the Scriptures and went on. I seen in my study notes, the Christ, but did not know Him. The Scriptures spoke about Him, but I only knew Him by name.

All that reading built me up to show me how great Christ is. When I got to the Gospel, surely I seen the greatness of Jesus. Firstly, the Words of Him in my text were red. He did many miracles. The devils cried out. The dead were raised. His teachings (them alone are profound, then the miracles?) But He died. Even stranger to me, He rose from the dead. I didn’t understand anything. He sends out His disciples.

I skipped the other three Gospels and went to the book of Acts. The disciples did the same things that He did. They preached. I still did not understand. All the while I was constantly getting on my knees to God in prayer because of my sin, knowing my wicked deeds deserve wickedness and I was cursed as a dead man. The things which I didn’t want to do, I constantly did. I didn’t want to do it, but I did it anyway. I had no power in myself to say, ‘No’ and sin was always there. Many times I thought God would strike me because of my wickedness, especially when I would confess my sins to Him; but actually, the opposite happened: I was relieved and felt better. But still, I couldn't stop sinning. I would go a few days without it, but would always end up back in it.

I heard the apostle’s sermons about Christ, and seen the effect the Word had on people. They burned their sorcery books. They turned from their idols. Paul, a preacher, once a persecutor of the church, turned into a preacher by revelation of the living Christ. However, the whole message wasn’t contained in the book, only a small excerpt of it -- a little here and a little there. I still didn’t understand. However, I sought Christ until I found Him. I said to myself, “There’s something about Jesus and I will not let go”.

Then the book of Romans. The apostle Paul explains Christ. I could trust his testimony because of what the Lord did on the road to Damascus. When reading, that’s when Christ came alive to me. I seen Christ came for the very problem I had: sin. He came to deliver me from the very sin I had. He didn't expect me to figure it out on my own, but He did what I could not do on my own. And because of the revelation of the love of Him that He showed me, despite my present wickedness, I wept and wept (for I had not wept in years).

Verses Romans 7:14-25, spoke to me directly for that was exactly what I was going through. I didn’t know what I was going through. But God did, and He showed me. For He knows me better than I know me. Only I know me. But if there is a God, He would know me. He shows me that He knows me. And that, better than I know me. For the things in me I cannot explain. But He explains. And that, better than I could ever explain. He delivered me from that which I could not understand. If I couldn't even understand my issue, how much less could I deliver myself? He set me upright on my feet. I believed nothing, then I believed one thing, and then I believed everything. For I said to myself, “If this is true, so is everything else in this Book.” The one thing I believed is that Jesus Christ loved me and gave Himself for me unto death. And He proved Himself to be alive.

From then, I didn’t cry out because of my sins anymore, but I had joy that He had forgiven me and delivered me from my sins. I experienced deliverance. The sin I didn’t want to do anymore, I did not do it. The chains fell off. I had a clear mind and I could see what was in there. As I went on, all the commands of the apostles were sweet to me, because I did them without first being told: be full of joy, be thankful, sing to the Lord, live holy, tell others, study, separate from evil, all these things I did because of my love for Him. I had in me afterwards an anger, "Why didn’t anyone tell me? This death I was living in and no-one warned me?" I looked outside and everything I seen for years became new to me. I seen God in everything. Everything was beautiful. The glory of the Lord filled the earth to my eyes. Before I could see nothing.

Now, I have been studying for myself to know more about what happened; and to know more about Christ. I know that this is Christ’s work in me; and I know that what has happened to me needs to be replicated in others. If I wish for others to be saved, they must seek the same One I experienced. He teaches me how to speak for Him and live for Him. The more I learn about Him, who is Good, the more evil I see in me, who is not good. In repentance & peace, I conform my life after His, following His Way & not making for myself my own. I was changed by God, and who can change me back? If some think I am crazy now, what will they think in five or ten years? If others think I am in my right mind, it is for their benefit.
