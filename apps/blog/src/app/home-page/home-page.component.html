The Good News of The Kingdom of God
The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a king who gave a wedding feast for his son, and sent his servants to call those who were invited to the wedding feast, but they would not come. Again he sent other servants, saying, ‘Tell those who are invited, “See, I have prepared my dinner, my oxen and my fat calves have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding feast.”’ But they paid no attention and went off, one to his farm, another to his business, while the rest seized his servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them. The king was angry, and he sent his troops and destroyed those murderers and burned their city. Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding feast is ready, but those invited were not worthy. Go therefore to the main roads and invite to the wedding feast as many as you find.’ And those servants went out into the roads and gathered all whom they found, both bad and good. So the wedding hall was filled with guests. But when the king came in to look at the guests, he saw there a man who had no wedding garment. And he said to him, Friend, how did you get in here without a wedding garment?’ And he was speechless. Then the king said to the attendants, ‘Bind him hand and foot and cast him into the outer darkness. In that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ For many are called, but few are chosen.

The Danger - Futility

Self Indulgence

Alcohol, drugs, food and all other pleasures under the sun do not sustain us. They make us happy for a time, but they do not make us happy forever. All the pleasures of the world would not be enough. We eat and we are not content; we drink and we are not sustained. We eat and are hungry again; we drink and are thirsty again. No matter how much the eye sees, it is not enough; No matter how much the ear hears, it is not content. If this kind of pleasure truly makes us happy, why don't we express it? If we are truly happy, shouldn't our happiness resemble a child's happiness? Children are overcome with laughter and express joyfulness and run around expressing their happiness. If these self indulgences truly make us happy, why don't we show it? All things are full of weariness; a man cannot utter it; the eye is not satisfied with seeing, nor the ear filled with hearing. What has been is what will be, and what has been done is what will be done, and there is nothing new under the sun. Is there a thing of which it is said, “See, this is new”? It has been already in the ages before us. There is no remembrance of former things, nor will there be any remembrance of later things yet to be among those who come after. We live in an age where media is everywhere. It started with televisions in the living room then escalated to monitors in our pockets. Is this a bad thing? The human eye consists of 3 cones responsible for color vision: red, green and blue; in the same way, these screens are made up of a pixels with these 3 colors. This is why only humans can see a computer screen or television screen as intended -- it minics the human eye. So man learned how God created the eye and then created colored TV and phones the same way. So while we look through our eyes, we also look through the eyes of a screen. Whatever another person wants you to see, you will see. And blessed are your eyes for seeing this. So you can see that this concept can be used for good or evil. Therefore, be watchful as you watch from your eyes. I said in my heart, “Come now, I will test you with pleasure; enjoy yourself.” But behold, this also was vanity. I said of laughter, “It is mad,” and of pleasure, “What use is it?” I searched with my heart how to cheer my body with wine—my heart still guiding me with wisdom—and how to lay hold on folly, till I might see what was good for the children of man to do under heaven during the few days of their life. (...) And whatever my eyes desired I did not keep from them. I kept my heart from no pleasure, for my heart found pleasure in all my toil, and this was my reward for all my toil. Then I considered all that my hands had done and the toil I had expended in doing it, and behold, all was vanity and a striving after wind, and there was nothing to be gained under the sun.

Toil

Our lives are filled with work. We work and sleep just to do it all over again. And for what? We recieve wages for our toil, but we have no time to spend the money we have earned. Even then, our money is given right back in the form of bills and taxes. Our days are short yet we spend them in pain and agony from our much work. We work for things that will not matter in the future; we work for objectives that will not matter years from now. In the future, people will look back and won't care what we are currently doing. Yet, we strive and labor and don't recieve our due wages. Instead, the boss does little work and eats the fruits of our labor. How meaningless! I hated all my toil in which I toil under the sun, seeing that I must leave it to the man who will come after me, and who knows whether he will be wise or a fool? Yet he will be master of all for which I toiled and used my wisdom under the sun. This also is vanity. So I turned about and gave my heart up to despair over all the toil of my labors under the sun, because sometimes a person who has toiled with wisdom and knowledge and skill must leave everything to be enjoyed by someone who did not toil for it. This also is vanity and a great evil. What has a man from all the toil and striving of heart with which he toils beneath the sun? For all his days are full of sorrow, and his work is a vexation. Even in the night his heart does not rest. This also is vanity. Life is short and we spend our lives working for other people's objectives; their will is to get rich and spend their lives in luxury. What then? Should we not work? Of course not! Do you think a doctor enjoys an abundant life thinking everything is so serious? He goes in and out of surgery, working hard all the days of his life, but hardly has time to enjoy his family. His surgeries are meticulous; his hands are steady; his mind is clear. The worse case scenario is death; the best case scenario is a life saved. If a doctor is so steady handed in these cases, why do we think so seriously of our jobs?

Wealth

The futility of life and wealth is like a man who goes into a pub with everything he owns. He sits down at a gambling table to roll dice. At first, he bets only a little bit of what he owns. He rolls the dice and a miracle happens! All the dice line up. The man yells, 'Today is my lucky day. I will bet more!' So he rolls again but with more of his possessions on the table. A miracle happens again! The dice all line up. The man continues this over and over many times; eventually, he has everything he owns on the table even the shirt off his back! He thinks to himself, 'Surely I have rolled these dice over and over and they haven't failed me!' However, the man handing out the dice sees the man and thinks, 'You fool! One day these dice will not line up and everything you have will be mine. For I have worked a long time in this pub and I see it happen over and over.' Then the day came when the dice did not line up and everything on the table was taken from the man. Because the man didn't even have enough money for a beer, he was then cast out of the pub into darkenss. He who loves money will not be satisfied with money, nor he who loves wealth with his income; this also is vanity. When goods increase, they increase who eat them, and what advantage has their owner but to see them with his eyes? Sweet is the sleep of a laborer, whether he eats little or much, but the full stomach of the rich will not let him sleep. There is a grievous evil that I have seen under the sun: riches were kept by their owner to his hurt, and those riches were lost in a bad venture. And he is father of a son, but he has nothing in his hand. As he came from his mother's womb he shall go again, naked as he came, and shall take nothing for his toil that he may carry away in his hand. This also is a grievous evil: just as he came, so shall he go, and what gain is there to him who toils for the wind? Moreover, all his days he eats in darkness in much vexation and sickness and anger. Look around! Point to something that you can take with you when you leave this earth; not even your own body will go with you. In the beginning, we were created from the dust of the earth; in the end, we will return to dust. In the beginning, our spirits were breathed into our bodies and we became living souls; in the end, our spirits will return to God who gave them. Train yourself for godliness; for while bodily training is of some value, godliness is of value in every way, as it holds promise for the present life and also for the life to come.

Death

This present life is like a man who lives in a tent for a little while. He is in anguish in his tent while he is away from home. In his permenant home he has a bed and kitchen and bathroom, but in his tent, he has a cot and dirt and a shovel. The man hopes and looks forward to his permenant home and reads about it constantly. Then, all of a sudden, the day comes when his tent is taken down with the rushing of a wind and a violent storm. The tent is destroyed, so the man goes to his permenant home where he spends all the rest of his days. I concluded that God is testing us, to show us that we are no better than animals. After all, the same fate awaits human beings and animals alike. One dies just like the other. They are both the same kind of creature. A human being is no better off than an animal, because life has no meaning for either. They are both going to the same place — the dust. They both came from it; they will both go back to it. How can anyone be sure that the human spirit goes upwards while an animal's spirit goes down into the ground? Also, the hearts of the children of man are full of evil, and madness is in their hearts while they live, and after that they go to the dead. But he who is joined with all the living has hope, for a living dog is better than a dead lion. For the living know that they will die, but the dead know nothing, and they have no more reward, for the memory of them is forgotten. Their love and their hate and their envy have already perished, and forever they have no more share in all that is done under the sun. (...) Whatever your hand finds to do, do it with your might, for there is no work or thought or knowledge or wisdom in Sheol (the place of the dead), to which you are going. We only have one life to live; no one asked to be born; no one chose their family; no one chose the body they would have; no one chose what country they would be born into. We are given what we have; we have been delt cards that cannot be taken back. God formed all of us with a purpose for a time and place as this. Woe to him who strives with Him who formed him, a pot among earthen pots! Does the clay say to him who forms it, ‘What are you making?’ or ‘Your work has no handles’? Woe to him who says to a father, ‘What are you begetting?’ or to a woman, ‘With what are you in labor?’

The Danger - Sin

Consuming Fire

God is for us and not against us. He is Love and He is Good. Not a bad thought crosses His mind. It is impossible for Him to tell a lie. He loves truth and justice and righteousness, but hates evil and sin and wrong. He is Light and there is no darkness in Him at all. He does not share His glory with another and He never changes. He is now, was from the beginning, and is yet to come in the future. God is three and one: the Will of God or the Father, the Word of God or Jesus Christ, and the Power of God or the Holy Spirit. God is forever and His love is unfailing. Heaven is His throne and the earth is His footstool and His Spirit chooses to live in human hearts. God is everywhere and knows everything. He is the First and the Last, He has no beginning nor end. God is not taught good, He is good. God is not taught love, He is love. God was not taught anything and everything comes from Him -- the good and the bad. God is a consuming fire and His very name is Jealous; He is jealous of anything we put in His place in our life.

If a husband loves his wife and his wife cheats on him with another man, the husband is going to feel two very strong emotions: anger and jealousy. Are the emotions in this scenario evil? No. They are powerful emotions that come from the husband's love for his wife. If the husband did not love his wife, then he would not feel anger or jealousy. Also, if a father has a child and the child continually plays with dangerous things like snakes and knives. The father will discipline the child and start to hate the snakes and knives which cause his child harm. Is the discipline done because the father hates the child? Of course not! In fact, the discipline is proof that the father loves his child and it's a sign that the father considers the child his own. If the father did not discipline the child, that would mean the child is illegitimate and a bastard. The father's hatred is directed towards the things that cause his child danger because he loves him. Everything is God's and He is good all the time; His love does not fail.

He Sees and Hears

God is living and sees the thoughts and intentions of every human heart, even when we ourselves do not know our own motives or intentions. We may think in our hearts, 'My motives are right. I will stand before God and He will agree.' Are our thoughts, God's thoughts? Are our ways, God's ways? As the heavens are higher than the earth, so are God's ways higher than your ways and His thoughts higher than your thoughts. I do not even judge myself. For I am not aware of anything against myself, but I am not thereby acquitted. It is the Lord who judges me. Therefore do not pronounce judgment before the time, before the Lord comes, who will bring to light the things now hidden in darkness and will disclose the purposes of the heart. Do we think we can hide from God? Is there any place on earth where He isn't? Isn't His eyes everywhere? In God's throne room, His mighty Cheribum are covered with eyes. Not eyes that are blind; but eyes that can see. Not eyes like we humans have, not eyes like an eagle which can spot a worm from miles away -- no. God seen what was happening below on Mount Sinai. God seen where Jonah went to get away from God. If that is not enough, He also sends His Watchers, which are mighty angels to look after us. He who planted the ear, does he not hear? He who formed the eye, does he not see? He who disciplines the nations, does he not rebuke? He who teaches man knowledge — the LORD — knows the thoughts of man, that they are but a breath. The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. And the LORD regretted that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart.

Our Sin Nature

Does anyone continually do good? Is anyone of us truly seeking God? How many of us do good all the time? Who speaks with integrity all the time? Which one of us does the right thing and chooses good rather than evil? Who is upright among us? Surley, it's not the one typing this. None is righteous, no, not one; no one understands; no one seeks for God. All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one. Their throat is an open grave; they use their tongues to deceive. The venom of asps is under their lips. Their mouth is full of curses and bitterness. Their feet are swift to shed blood; in their paths are ruin and misery, and the way of peace they have not known. There is no fear of God before their eyes. A new born baby is precious; but, is the baby good? If we are good, then why do we need to be taught good? If we were good, then we wouldn't need to be taught good; but it is true that we inheritly know bad; we inherit this 'knowing' from the first man, Adam. In the garden, he sinned against God and was decieved by Eve; so, since one man sinned once, all became sinners. As a child we are taught good and we already know evil - that which is evil we don't need to learn, but that which is good we need to learn. This is our nature; we don't need convincing; it's effortless for us to do bad and takes all our energy to do good. As we grow old and we are not taught good, our parents are blamed. But why? People say, 'I blame the parents for the child's behavior' or 'Why didn't the parents teach them what is right?'. Therefore, we testify to ourselves about the way we are. Even more we say, 'I am only human' when we do wrong. This confirms to ourselves and others that we belong to a nature that is fallen and broken. It is our nature as it's an eagle's nature to fly. The trouble is not with the law, for it is spiritual and good. The trouble is with me, for I am all too human, a slave to sin. I don’t really understand myself, for I want to do what is right, but I don’t do it. Instead, I do what I hate. But if I know that what I am doing is wrong, this shows that I agree that the law is good. So I am not the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. And I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my sinful nature. I want to do what is right, but I can’t. I want to do what is good, but I don’t. I don’t want to do what is wrong, but I do it anyway. But if I do what I don’t want to do, I am not really the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. I have discovered this principle of life-—that when I want to do what is right, I inevitably do what is wrong. I love God’s law with all my heart. But there is another power within me that is at war with my mind. This power makes me a slave to the sin that is still within me. Oh, what a miserable person I am! Who will free us from this life that is dominated by sin and death? Thank God! The answer is in Jesus Christ our Lord.

The Day of Judgement

Everything done in our body will need to be accounted for on Judgement Day. This means every Work and every Word will need to be accounted for. God is the Just Judge and He is so just that He cannot let anyone go unpunished. If He let just one go unpunished, He wouldn't be Good and Just. People in this world go and riot when justice is not upheld. When someone who is supposed to be punished for their crimes gets off free, they go out and riot against the judge and police and say, 'You are no good! Off with your head!' God is not like any judge on earth. He will punish all evil. I will discipline you, but with justice; I cannot let you go unpunished. Therefore, since everyone of us is sinful and God sees and hears everything that everyone does and God will not let anyone go unpunished -- Who then can be saved? Aren't we all doomed? If there is no hope, then, "Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die". Isn't the danger so evident? What must we do to be saved from the wrath of God? What must we do to be free'd from the power of sin and death? (The command) is not in heaven, that you should say, ‘Who will ascend to heaven for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?’ Neither is it beyond the sea, that you should say, ‘Who will go over the sea for us and bring it to us, that we may hear it and do it?’ But the word is very near you. It is in your mouth and in your heart, so that you can do it. The world thinks that the truth is hidden: in the depths of the ocean, in outer space, or in the center of the earth. They spend countless thousands of dollars to explore where no man has gone before. They build towers up into heaven to reach God just as they did in times past at the Tower of Babel. They do not know, but you now know: The Truth has came and the Truth has left and the Truth sent His Spirit and the Truth will come again. Who will free us from this life that is dominated by sin and death? Thank God! The answer is in Jesus Christ our Lord.
