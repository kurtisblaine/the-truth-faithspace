Holy, Holy, Holy is He

My name is Kurtis. Below is the testimony the Lord gave me. Use the navigation in the top left corner. Click here to start reading Scripture. It was Covid-19, I had just got my full time job, experienced military life and college. What was left but to get married, have kids and die? I said to myself, “Surely life isn’t here, but death, but where?” I started seeking. I figured man doesn’t know, if they did, wouldn’t they tell me? But all my life no-one told me.

I do have a memory of going to church: specifically that my step dad repeated the Lord’s prayer; I was surprised that he knew it in his heart. Another memory I had was of my aunt in the Hutterite colony praying with us and the delight I knew from God in it and her testimony of the holiness of God: for we would say things like “holy cow” as kids, and she would say, “cows aren’t holy”. Then we would ‘know better’ and say, “holy moley” then she would say, “aren’t holy”. We did this for awhile, then she told us that 'God is Holy'.

I figured the churches in America who are claiming God aren’t trying to convince me of the truth, so they didn’t know it; for the one who is convinced themselves, tries to convince others. I also seen that it was very popular as it was on almost every street. So I said to myself, “Surely if the truth exists, only few would know it”. For is not the truth a mystery?

Now writing this and looking back: Oh, the dangers of hypocrisy! And the reward of speaking simply the truth! For by our lives, we cause others to stumble and by our lives, we edify in the paths of uprightness. The things written which I remember when I was a child pertaining to God have stood with me and have encouraged me, showing me that He has always been with me. And in all these things, although I did not know any of these things, the Lord has been presently revealing my heart to me during that time.

I started to open my Bible. My mom had given it to me. She was on a journey herself with God and gave all her things to the poor. I said “What? This is from God?” (The only other time I remember opening my Bible was when my step dad died and right before leaving for the military). Then she moved out of the state. That made me want to seek all the more. So I opened up my Bible and remembered the change it had on my life; although the change was quick, it wasn’t drastic, I had peace and other spiritual things, but I was still in sin.

I remember doing much work at my new job and reading Scripture. I remember praying to God, “Ah! If only I could seek much more” I don’t know if it was an answer to my prayers or not, but Covid-19 happened and I was put in solitude, working from home. So I read. The Scripture that opened my eyes up was Ecclesiastes. I seen the wisdom and the futility of life. Also the Psalms. I had very much conviction and judgement because of my sin. And Isaiah. The revealing of my own wicked heart. For I started at the beginning of the Scriptures and went on. I seen in my study notes, Christ, but did not know Him. The Scriptures spoke about Him, but I only knew Him by name.

All that reading built me up to show me how great Christ is. When I got to the Gospel, surely I seen the greatness of Jesus. Firstly, the Words of Him in my text were red. Then He did many miracles. The devils cried out. The dead were raised. His teachings (them alone are profound, then miracles?). But He died. Even stranger to me, He rose from the dead. I didn’t understand anything. He sends out His disciples.

I skipped the other three Gospels and went to the book of Acts. The disciples did the same things that He did. They preached. I still did not understand. All the while I was constantly getting on my knees to God in prayer because of my sin, knowing my wicked deeds deserve wickedness and I was cursed as a dead man. The things which I didn’t want to do, I constantly did. I didn’t want it, but I did it anyway. I had no power in myself to say ‘No’. Many of the times I thought God would strike me because of my wickedness when I would confess my sins to Him; but afterwards, the opposite happened, I was relieved.

I heard the apostle’s sermons about Christ, and seen the effect it had on people. They burned their magic books. They turned from idols. However, the whole message wasn’t contained in the book, only a small excerpt of it, a little here and a little there. I still didn’t understand. However, I sought Christ until I found Him. I said to myself, “There’s something about Jesus and I will not let go”.

Then the book of Romans. The apostle explains Christ. That’s when Christ came alive to me. I seen Christ came for the very problem I had: sin. He came to deliver me from the very sin I had and didn’t want to do anymore. Because of the love of Him unto me even in my wickedness, I kept weeping and weeping.

Verses Romans 7:14-25, spoke to me directly during that time for that was exactly what I was going through. I didn’t know I was going through. But God did, and He showed me. For He knows me better than I know me. Only I know me. But if there is a God, He would know me. He showed me that He knew me. And that, better than I know me. For the things in me I cannot explain. But He explains. And that, better than I could ever explain. He delivered me from that which I did not understand. Then He set me on my feet. I believed nothing, then I believed one thing, and I believed everything. For I said to myself, “If this is true, so is everything else!” The one thing I believed is that Christ Jesus loved me and gave Himself for me unto death. And He proved to be alive.

From then I didn’t cry out because of my sins anymore, but I had joy He had forgiven me and delivered me from my sins. I experienced deliverance. The sin I didn’t want to do anymore, I could say “No” and not do it. The chains fell off. I had a clear mind and I could see what was in there. I could say “No” and resist. As I went on, all the commands of the apostles were sweet to me, because I did them without being told: be full of joy, be thankful, sing to the Lord, live holy, tell others, study, all these I did because of the love of Him. I had in me afterwards an anger. For why didn’t anyone tell me? This death I was living in; no-one warned me.

Now, I have been studying for myself to know what has happened to me; for I know I have been changed. I know that this is Christ’s doing. I also know that what has happened to me needs to happen to others. If I wish for others to be saved, they must experience something in the manner of what I have experienced. And I must learn how to tell it. I have come to think of what I’ve experienced to mean what it is to be born again.
