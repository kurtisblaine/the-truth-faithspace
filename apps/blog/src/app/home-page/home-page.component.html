The Good News of The Kingdom of God

“The kingdom of heaven may be compared to a king who gave a wedding feast for his son, and sent his servants to call those who were invited to the wedding feast, but they would not come. Again he sent other servants, saying, ‘Tell those who are invited, “See, I have prepared my dinner, my oxen and my fat calves have been slaughtered, and everything is ready. Come to the wedding feast.”’ But they paid no attention and went off, one to his farm, another to his business, while the rest seized his servants, treated them shamefully, and killed them. The king was angry, and he sent his troops and destroyed those murderers and burned their city. Then he said to his servants, ‘The wedding feast is ready, but those invited were not worthy. Go therefore to the main roads and invite to the wedding feast as many as you find.’ And those servants went out into the roads and gathered all whom they found, both bad and good. So the wedding hall was filled with guests. But when the king came in to look at the guests, he saw there a man who had no wedding garment. And he said to him, Friend, how did you get in here without a wedding garment?’ And he was speechless. Then the king said to the attendants, ‘Bind him hand and foot and cast him into the outer darkness. In that place there will be weeping and gnashing of teeth.’ For many are called, but few are chosen.”

The Danger

God is a Consuming Fire

God is for us and not against us. He is Love and He is Good. Not a bad thought crosses His mind. It is impossible for Him to tell a lie. He loves truth and justice and righteousness, but hates evil and sin and wrong. He is Light and there is no darkness in Him at all. He does not share His glory with another and He never changes. He is now, was from the beginning, and is yet to come in the future. God is three, yet One God: the Will of God or the Father, the Word of God or Jesus Christ, and the Power of God or the Holy Spirit. God is forever and His love is unfailing. Heaven is His throne and the earth is His footstool and His Spirit chooses to live in human hearts (our inner most being or spirit). God is everywhere and knows everything. He is the First and the Last, yet He has no beginning nor end. God is not taught good, but He is good. God is not taught love, but He is love. God was not taught anything and everything comes from Him - the good and the bad. God is a consuming fire and His very name is Jealous. He is jealous of anything we put in His place in our life. If a husband loves his wife and his wife cheats on him with another man. The husband is going to feel two very strong emotions: anger and jealousy. Are these emotions evil? No. They are powerful emotions that come from the husband's love for his wife. If the husband did not love his wife, then he would not feel anger or jealousy. Also, if a father has a child and the child continually plays with snakes or knives. The father will discipline the child and start to hate the snakes and knives. Is the discipline done because the father hates the child? Of course not! In fact, the discipline is proof that the father loves the child. The father's hatred is directed towards the things that cause his child danger. All of creation is His. Even when we are in the worst situations, God's love does not fail to those who love Him.

God Sees and Hears Everything

God sees that every man is corrupt. “None is righteous, no, not one; no one understands; no one seeks for God. All have turned aside; together they have become worthless; no one does good, not even one. Their throat is an open grave; they use their tongues to deceive. The venom of asps is under their lips. Their mouth is full of curses and bitterness. Their feet are swift to shed blood; in their paths are ruin and misery, and the way of peace they have not known. There is no fear of God before their eyes." Does anyone continually do good? Is anyone of us truly seeking God? "He who planted the ear, does he not hear? He who formed the eye, does he not see? He who disciplines the nations, does he not rebuke? He who teaches man knowledge — the LORD — knows the thoughts of man, that they are but a breath." “Nothing in all creation is hidden from God’s sight. Everything is uncovered and laid bare before the eyes of Him to whom we must give account.” and "The LORD saw that the wickedness of man was great in the earth, and that every intention of the thoughts of his heart was only evil continually. And the LORD regretted that he had made man on the earth, and it grieved him to his heart."

Our Sin Nature

A new born baby is precious. But if we are good, then why do we need to be taught good at a young age? We inheritly know evil, but why don't we know good? As a child we are taught good, but we already know evil - that which is evil we don't need to learn. As we grow older and are not taught good, our parents are blamed. People will say, 'I blame the parents for the child's behavior' or 'Why didn't their parents teach them what is right'. There is a sin nature that we have all been born with. People of the world say, 'I am only human' when they do something wrong. This confirms to themselves and others that they have a nature that is fallen and broken. This is the sin nature: "The trouble is not with the law, for it is spiritual and good. The trouble is with me, for I am all too human, a slave to sin. I don’t really understand myself, for I want to do what is right, but I don’t do it. Instead, I do what I hate. But if I know that what I am doing is wrong, this shows that I agree that the law is good. So I am not the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. And I know that nothing good lives in me, that is, in my sinful nature. I want to do what is right, but I can’t. I want to do what is good, but I don’t. I don’t want to do what is wrong, but I do it anyway. But if I do what I don’t want to do, I am not really the one doing wrong; it is sin living in me that does it. I have discovered this principle of life-—that when I want to do what is right, I inevitably do what is wrong. I love God’s law with all my heart. But there is another power within me that is at war with my mind. This power makes me a slave to the sin that is still within me. Oh, what a miserable person I am!"

The Day of Judgement

Everything done in our body will need to be accounted for on Judgement Day. That means every work and every word you will need to be discussed. God is the Just Judge and He is so just that He cannot let anyone go unpunished. If He let just one go unpunished, He wouldn't be the Good and Just God. People in this world go and riot when justice is not upheld. When someone who is supposed to be judged for their crimes gets off free they go out and riot against the judge and police and say, 'You are no good!' God is not like any judge on earth. He will punish all evil for that is who He is. He is Good and He is Just. Therefore, since everyone of us is sinful and God sees and hears everything that everyone does and God will not let anyone go unpunished -- Who then can be saved? Aren't we all doomed? If there is no hope, then “Let us eat and drink, for tomorrow we die.” "Who will free us from this life that is dominated by sin and death? Thank God! The answer is in Jesus Christ our Lord."
