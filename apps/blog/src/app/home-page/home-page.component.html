My wish is that you would be encouraged by this message. Click the header of the section to hear commentating as I'm helping you along as a servant of Christ, even this I am unworthy of. May God bless you and reveal the grace of His Son to you and keep you from evil. We don't know where we came from: we can't say what we did a day ago; We don't know where we are going: we can't say what will happen tomorrow. All we know is today: all we know is right now. Today is the day of salvation; and right now is the time of God's favor. When you hear His voice, do not harden your hearts as the Israelites did in the rebellion in the wilderness where they tested God for 40 years although they seen His great works. Let us remember the root of all disobedience and sin is unbelief; so therefore, let us re-assure ourselves with the confidence which comes from faith in Jesus Christ: He died for us, and was buried, and was raised, and now lives forever, seated at the right hand of the Father in heaven. Let us remember what faith is: faith is sight without seeing; the conviction of things unseen; the assurance of things hoped for.

Let us remember the exhortations and exclamations spoken of by the Lord to those who would hear and believe His Word, producing in themselves a faith that was very great, having themselves marveled the Lord by their faith, and calling to Him for deliverance because they believed He is powerful and able to do. As it is written, And when Jesus entered Capernaum, a centurion came to Him, imploring Him, and saying, “Lord, my servant is lying paralyzed at home, fearfully tormented.” Jesus said to him, “I will come and heal him.” But the centurion said, “Lord, I am not worthy for You to come under my roof, but just say the word, and my servant will be healed. Now when Jesus heard this, He marveled and said to those who were following, “Truly I say to you, I have not found such great faith with anyone in Israel. But He answered and said, “I was sent only to the lost sheep of the house of Israel.” But she came and began to bow down before Him, saying, “Lord, help me!” And He answered and said, “It is not good to take the children’s bread and throw it to the dogs.” But she said, “Yes, Lord; but even the dogs feed on the crumbs which fall from their masters’ table.” Then Jesus said to her, “O woman, your faith is great; it shall be done for you as you wish.” And her daughter was healed at once.
Let us, on the other hand, remember the Lord's words to those who doubted in times past and let us not shrink back in disbelief or fear, but instead, be strengthen by God's grace moving forward, fearlessly, towards the heavenly prize which is ours in Christ Jesus. He reprimanded many because of their little faith, as it is written, if God so clothes the grass of the field, which is alive today and tomorrow is thrown into the furnace, will He not much more clothe you? You of little faith! And behold, there arose a great storm on the sea, so that the boat was being covered with the waves; but Jesus Himself was asleep. And they came to Him and woke Him, saying, “Save us, Lord; we are perishing!” He said to them, "Why are you afraid, you men of little faith?” Then He got up and rebuked the winds and the sea, and it became perfectly calm. Peter said to Him, “Lord, if it is You, command me to come to You on the water.” And He said, “Come!” And Peter got out of the boat, and walked on the water and came toward Jesus. But seeing the wind, he became frightened, and beginning to sink, he cried out, “Lord, save me!” Immediately Jesus stretched out His hand and took hold of him, and said to him, “You of little faith, why did you doubt?” When they came to the crowd, a man came up to Jesus, falling on his knees before Him and saying, “Lord, have mercy on my son, for he is a lunatic and is very ill; for he often falls into the fire and often into the water. I brought him to Your disciples, and they could not cure him.” And Jesus answered and said, "You unbelieving and perverse generation, how long shall I be with you? How long shall I put up with you?"
Finally, let us keep in mind, what is needed is a faith the size of a mustard seed. If Christ reprimanded them on their little faith, then how little that faith must have been! Let us consider the prophet Elijah who is a human like us; he prayed that it would not rain and it did not rain for three and a half years. Then he prayed again for rain and it rained just as he asked. Let us beware of doubt which is the killer of faith. Doubt or confusion is the product of being double minded or impure from the blending together of falsehood and truth, the world and the Kingdom of God. It has often thrown him both into the fire and into the water to destroy him. But if You can do anything, take pity on us and help us!” And Jesus said to him, “‘If You can?’ All things are possible to him who believes.” Immediately the boy’s father cried out and said, “I do believe; help my unbelief.” Then the disciples came to Jesus privately and said, “Why could we not drive it out?” And He said to them, “Because of the littleness of your faith; for truly I say to you, if you have faith the size of a mustard seed, you will say to this mountain, ‘Move from here to there,’ and it will move; and nothing will be impossible to you.
